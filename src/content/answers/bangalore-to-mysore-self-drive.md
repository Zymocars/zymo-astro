    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Bangalore to Mysore self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Bangalore to Mysore is 150 km via the Mysore Expressway (NH275), approximately 2.5 hours. The Mysore Expressway is one of India's best 4-lane roads — smooth, well-lit, FASTag managed. Toll: ₹120 one-way for a hatchback. A hatchback handles this route perfectly — Mysore city sightseeing is easier in a smaller car. Budget: car ₹1,100-2,200/day + fuel ₹1,750 round trip.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Route and what to see in Mysore</h2><p>Bangalore → Mandya via NH275 (100 km, 1.5 hrs): excellent 4-lane expressway with a toll plaza at Malavalli. Mandya → Mysore (50 km, 45 mins): continues NH275. Mysore city sightseeing: Mysore Palace (centrepiece, 45-minute interior tour, ₹100 entry), Chamundi Hill (12 km, temple at top, views of city), Brindavan Gardens (23 km from palace, Krishnarajasagar Dam — illuminated Sunday evenings), St Philomena's Church (100-year-old Gothic structure), Mysore Zoo (one of India's best, ₹100 entry). Return Mysore-Bangalore same day or overnight.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Bangalore-Mysore self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,100-1,500/day. Day trip: ₹1,100-1,500. Fuel: 300 km round trip; hatchback at 15 km/L = ₹2,100. Tolls: ₹120 one-way (₹240 return) at Malavalli plaza. Mysore Palace entry: ₹100 per person (plus ₹50 for camera). Brindavan Gardens: ₹30 entry per person. Total day-trip budget for 2 people: ₹5,000-7,500 — one of the best-value road trips from Bangalore.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Mysore sightseeing circuit by self-drive car</h2><p>Mysore Palace: start here — opens 10 AM, closes 5:30 PM daily. Parking lot is ₹30/car. Chamundi Hill: 12 km drive up — temple at 1,001m elevation. Drive up, darshan, drive back (1.5 hours). Brindavan Gardens: 23 km from palace — sunset light illuminations are best; musical fountain show at 7 PM (book tickets online at karnatakaecotourism.in). Mysore Zoo: 2 km from palace — allow 2 hours for a proper visit. Srirangapatna: 15 km from Mysore — Tipu Sultan's island fortress. Somnathpur Hoysala Temple: 38 km from Mysore — UNESCO-nominated exquisitely carved stone temple.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Bangalore airport area (NH275 start, Hebbal): fill up here before the expressway. Mandya (100 km from Bangalore): BPCL and HPCL stations near the NH275-Mandya city junction — good midpoint if needed. Mysore city outskirts (Hebbal, Kuvempunagar): multiple fuel stations before entering the city. Road conditions: Bangalore-Mysore NH275 is India's best 4-lane road in this range — excellent surface, well-lit, speed cameras at Malavalli (80 km/h zone); no ghat difficulty, fully flat.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Bangalore-Mysore on Zymo</h2><p>Maruti Swift or Hyundai i20: the ideal Mysore trip car — flat expressway, easy parking in Mysore city's bazaar streets and monument approaches. You genuinely don't need an SUV for Mysore. Compact SUV: only warranted if you're extending past Mysore to Coorg (Hunsur ghat section) or Ooty (Bandipur-Masinagudi). Book from Koramangala, HSR Layout, or Electronic City partners on Zymo — all are close to the NH275 southward exit from Bangalore.</p></section>
