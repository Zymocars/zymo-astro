    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Bangalore to Mysore self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Bangalore to Mysore is about 145 km via the Bengaluru-Mysuru Expressway (NH275), approximately 3 hours. The expressway is access-controlled, smooth and FASTag-managed. Car toll: about ₹370 one way for a car (₹185 on each of the expressway's two tolled sections, as of October 2026). A hatchback handles this route perfectly — Mysore city sightseeing is easier in a smaller car. Budget: car ₹1,200-1,800/day (hatchback) + fuel ₹1,830 round trip.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Route and what to see in Mysore</h2><p>Bangalore → Mandya via NH275 (100 km, 1.5 hrs): access-controlled expressway, tolled in two sections. Mandya → Mysore (50 km, 45 mins): continues NH275. Mysore city sightseeing: Mysore Palace (centrepiece, 45-minute interior tour, ₹100 entry), Chamundi Hill (12 km, temple at top, views of city), Brindavan Gardens (23 km from palace, Krishnarajasagar Dam — illuminated Sunday evenings), St Philomena's Church (100-year-old Gothic structure), Mysore Zoo (one of India's oldest zoos). Return Mysore-Bangalore same day or overnight.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Bangalore-Mysore self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,200-1,800/day. Day trip: ₹1,200-1,800. Fuel: about 290 km round trip; a hatchback at 16 km/L and ₹101/L = about ₹1,830. Tolls: about ₹370 one way for a car (₹185 on each of the expressway's two tolled sections, as of October 2026) — about ₹740 round trip. Mysore Palace entry: ₹100 per person (plus ₹50 for camera). Brindavan Gardens: ₹30 entry per person. Total day-trip budget for 2 people: about ₹4,080-4,680 with tolls and entry tickets, before food.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Mysore sightseeing circuit by self-drive car</h2><p>Mysore Palace: start here — opens 10 AM, closes 5:30 PM daily. Parking lot is ₹30/car. Chamundi Hill: 12 km drive up — temple at 1,001m elevation. Drive up, darshan, drive back (1.5 hours). Brindavan Gardens: 23 km from palace — sunset light illuminations are best; musical fountain show in the evening (check current timings before you go). Mysore Zoo: 2 km from palace — allow 2 hours for a proper visit. Srirangapatna: 15 km from Mysore — Tipu Sultan's island fortress. Somnathpur Hoysala Temple: 38 km from Mysore — UNESCO World Heritage-listed, exquisitely carved stone temple.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Kengeri, on Mysore Road at the expressway start: fill up here. Mandya (100 km from Bangalore): BPCL and HPCL stations near the NH275-Mandya city junction — good midpoint if needed. Mysore city outskirts (Hebbal, Kuvempunagar): multiple fuel stations before entering the city. Road conditions: Bangalore-Mysore NH275 is a 6-lane expressway — excellent surface, with speed cameras along the route; no ghat difficulty, fully flat.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Bangalore-Mysore on Zymo</h2><p>Maruti Swift or Hyundai i20: the ideal Mysore trip car — flat expressway, easy parking in Mysore city's bazaar streets and monument approaches. You genuinely don't need an SUV for Mysore. Compact SUV: only warranted if you're extending past Mysore to Coorg (Hunsur ghat section) or Ooty (Bandipur-Masinagudi). Book from Koramangala, HSR Layout, or Electronic City partners on Zymo — all are close to the NH275 southward exit from Bangalore.</p></section>
