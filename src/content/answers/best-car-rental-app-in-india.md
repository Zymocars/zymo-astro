Single-operator apps show you their own fleet only, which limits your options and price comparison. Aggregator apps like Zymo pull inventory from multiple rental partners across 60+ cities and let you pick the best combination of price, pickup location, and vehicle for your exact dates. An aggregator app typically surfaces meaningful savings versus a single-operator app because you see the market rate for your dates, not one operator's price in isolation.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How aggregator apps beat single-operator apps</h2><p>A single-operator app is like using only one hotel chain's app to book a hotel — you see their inventory at their rates, without knowing what the room down the street costs on another platform. Aggregator car rental apps solve this by combining inventory from multiple rental operators into one search. For a Mumbai-Goa weekend trip, a single-operator app shows only its own cars at its own prices. An aggregator like Zymo shows cars from multiple partners at their competing prices, letting you pick the cheapest Swift at the closest pickup point rather than being locked into one operator's inventory. The comparison also surfaces hidden differences — some operators bundle insurance while others charge extra, some include unlimited kilometres while others cap at 200 km per day. The aggregator app's transparency reveals these before you book.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to look for in a car rental app</h2><p>Free to use with no platform fee added on top of the operator's price. Transparent pricing showing partner-by-partner rates side by side, including security deposit, kilometre limit, and fuel policy. Wide city and partner coverage — the more partners a platform aggregates, the deeper the price competition and inventory options. Verified partner list with real pickup addresses (not just city names). Aadhaar OTP verification and cashless payment via UPI, card, or net banking. Responsive support during active trips. Zymo aggregates multiple trusted partners across 60+ Indian cities with no platform fee and transparent partner-by-partner pricing — the price you see is the price you pay, and no hidden add-ons appear at checkout.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">When a single-operator app makes sense</h2><p>If you always rent from the same operator and are enrolled in their loyalty programme, their app may give you discounts or priority booking that aggregator apps do not replicate. Single-operator apps sometimes also list operator-exclusive vehicles (specific EVs, premium cars) that partner-facing aggregators may not surface. For everyone else — travellers who care about price and pickup convenience more than operator loyalty — an aggregator app delivers better outcomes by exposing the full market. The Zymo app on Android and iOS is a free way to see the whole market in one search before you decide.</p></section>
