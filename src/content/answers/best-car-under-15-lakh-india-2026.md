    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Best car under ₹15 lakh in India 2026 — a buyer's evaluation framework</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">By <a href="/founder" style="color:#4F7BFF;text-decoration:none">Manish Pratik</a>, Founder of Zymo &middot; June 2026</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Most 'best car under 15 lakh' articles compare spec sheets. I evaluate cars differently — as someone who runs extended test drives on Zymo and has seen what Indian buyers discover (and regret) after a few days with the actual car. The framework below is built around ownership, not just specifications.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The 5-dimension evaluation framework</h2><p>Real-world mileage (not ARAI): what owners in your city report on forums and YouTube. Ownership cost over 5 years: service interval costs, insurance premiums, tyre replacement, and spare parts availability. Boot space for the Indian family trip: can 4 people's cabin luggage plus a stroller fit? Rear seat experience for adults: the person in the rear row on a 4-hour drive matters as much as the driver. Resale value at year 5 in your specific city: Tier-2 city resale is meaningfully different from Metro resale for Korean brands.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The shortlist for 2026</h2><p>Compact SUV under ₹15L: Tata Nexon (209mm ground clearance, 5-star Global NCAP rating, EV option), Maruti Brezza (198mm ground clearance, a strong fuel-economy reputation), Kia Sonet (a feature-rich cabin). Hatchback under ₹15L: Hyundai i20 (a refined ride), Maruti Baleno (strong resale). Check current ex-showroom prices with dealers, since they change with taxes and model updates. The right choice depends on your usage pattern — city-only, highway-heavy, or family road trips are three different optimisation problems.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What spec sheet comparisons miss</h2><p>Dealer network density in your city matters. A Maruti dealer who can service your car next week vs a Kia dealer 25 km away changes the ownership experience materially. Spare parts pricing: Maruti parts typically cost less than Korean brands' from year 4 onward. The ownership cost gap widens after warranty ends. Service quality varies city by city — read owner reviews specific to your city's service centre, not national reviews. These factors are invisible in a spec sheet comparison and visible only in 3-5 year owner reviews.</p></section>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Related questions</h2>
    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the best car under 15 lakh in India in 2026?</dt>
    <dd>Depends on your priority. For safety and an EV option: Tata Nexon. For low running costs: Maruti Brezza. For a feature-rich cabin: Kia Sonet. For highway refinement: Hyundai Creta lower trims. Check current ex-showroom prices with dealers. Each wins on a different dimension — the best car is whichever dimension matters most to your actual use case.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is petrol or diesel better for a car under 15 lakh in 2026?</dt>
    <dd>Petrol for under 1,000 km per month or primarily city driving. Diesel makes sense financially above 1,500 km per month on long highway drives. In 2026, diesel options under ₹15L are narrowing — Maruti stopped selling diesel cars. Petrol-turbo and hybrid options have effectively replaced diesel in this segment for most buyers.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Should I buy a hatchback or compact SUV under 15 lakh?</dt>
    <dd>For an Indian family of 4, compact SUV wins: better ground clearance for speed bumps and flooded underpasses, bigger boot, more comfortable rear seat for adults on trips. The ground clearance difference — about 165mm hatchback vs 190mm SUV — matters on Indian roads. Hatchback wins only on tight parking and slightly better fuel economy.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What should I test on an extended test drive for a sub-15 lakh car?</dt>
    <dd>Park it in your actual housing society spot. Drive your commute in morning peak. Sit in the rear seat for 30 minutes on a highway. Test the infotainment with your own phone in direct sunlight. Load the boot with your real luggage. These tests cannot be replicated in a 30-minute showroom drive.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Which cars under 15 lakh have the best resale value?</dt>
    <dd>Maruti models such as the Baleno and Brezza are known for strong resale, with the Hyundai i20 and Creta close behind. Kia resale can vary more between metros and Tier-2 cities. Tata's resale has improved alongside its safety reputation. Check current used-car prices in your city.</dd>
