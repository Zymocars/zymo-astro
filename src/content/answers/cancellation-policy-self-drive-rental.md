    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How refunds are processed</h2>
    <p>Refunds for partner-side cancellations are credited back to your original payment method within 5-7 working days, plus 2-3 days of bank processing depending on your card or UPI provider. Total time from cancellation to money-in-account typically ranges 7-10 days.</p>
    <p>For Zymo bookings, the refund flow goes through Cashfree (the payment gateway). Track the status in your Zymo booking history — when the partner approves the cancellation, the refund is initiated automatically.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">If you're running late</h2>
    <p>If you anticipate being late for pickup, message the partner via Zymo to extend the pickup window. Most partners accommodate reasonable delays (up to 2-3 hours) without penalty if you communicate. Without communication, arriving more than 2 hours after the booked pickup time is treated as a no-show, and the partner forfeits the entire booking amount.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Shortening or extending bookings</h2>
    <p><strong>Shortening:</strong> Most partners refund the unused days minus a small early-return fee (typically ₹100-500 per day). Long-duration bookings (weekly or monthly) may lose their discount if shortened — you'd be charged at the daily rate for the actual days used. Confirm before agreeing to shorten.</p>
    <p><strong>Extending:</strong> Most partners accept extensions if requested before the original return time AND the car is available for the extended period. The extra days are charged at the original daily rate. Late returns without extension request typically incur a per-day late fee plus the standard daily rate — usually 50% more than the regular rate.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Force majeure scenarios</h2>
    <p>Most rental partners offer full refunds (or a credit for future use) when bookings are cancelled due to circumstances outside the renter's control — natural disasters, government-imposed lockdowns, severe weather warnings on the route, partner-side car unavailability. The partner-level policy on Zymo shows how each partner handles force-majeure cancellations. If in doubt, contact the partner directly via Zymo before cancelling.</p>
    </section>
