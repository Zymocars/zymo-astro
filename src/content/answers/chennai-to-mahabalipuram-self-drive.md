    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Chennai to Mahabalipuram is 55 km via the East Coast Road (ECR), approximately 1 hour. Mahabalipuram is a UNESCO World Heritage Site — Pallava dynasty rock-cut temples, the Shore Temple facing the Bay of Bengal, and the famous 5 Rathas. A hatchback handles the ECR perfectly. Budget: car ₹1,200 + fuel about ₹770, plus any ECR tolls.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Route and what to see at Mahabalipuram</h2><p>Chennai (OMR-ECR junction, Sholinganallur or Perungudi) → Mahabalipuram (55 km, 1 hr): East Coast Road is 4-lane with Bay of Bengal on the right. Mahabalipuram monuments: Shore Temple (UNESCO, ASI ticket — sea-facing granite temple, built around 700 CE), Five Rathas (monolithic rock-cut temple chariots, same ASI ticket), Arjuna's Penance (one of the world's largest bas-relief rock carvings, free), Varaha Cave (rock-cut cave temple, free). Crocodile Bank (about 45 km from Chennai, before Mahabalipuram): India's first crocodile conservation centre. Total sightseeing time: 3-4 hours for all sites.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Chennai-Mahabalipuram self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,200-1,700/day. Day trip: ₹1,200-1,700. Fuel: 110 km round trip; hatchback at 15 km/L = ₹770. ECR tolls, where charged, are paid via FASTag. ASI monument entry: ₹40 per person covers both the Shore Temple and the Five Rathas. Car, fuel, and monument tickets for 2 people: about ₹2,050-2,550.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">ECR driving highlights and tips</h2><p>East Coast Road from Chennai is one of India's most scenic 4-lane coastal roads — the ocean is visible from multiple points between km 20 and km 55. Kovalam beach (35 km): turquoise fishing village beach with a lighthouse. Fisherman's Cove/Covelong (40 km): resort beach, watersports available. Mahabalipuram town: park at the main car park near Shore Temple — the temple zone is walkable from the lot. Best time to visit: early morning (7-9 AM) before busloads of tourists arrive; the Shore Temple at sunrise over the Bay of Bengal is exceptional.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Sholinganallur (15 km from Chennai, before ECR starts): fill up here — ECR has limited fuel stations. OMR-ECR junction: if coming from the south OMR, the Indian Oil station at the junction is the last city-fuel before the coastal road. Mahabalipuram town: fuel station on the ECR. Road conditions: the ECR from Chennai to Mahabalipuram is a 4-lane coastal highway — well-maintained, no ghat.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Chennai-Mahabalipuram on Zymo</h2><p>Hatchback (i20, Swift, WagonR): absolutely the right car — ECR is flat and well-surfaced, Mahabalipuram monument parking is tight for larger vehicles. Compact SUV: not warranted for this route unless combining with a longer Pondicherry extension. Book from OMR, Velachery, or Anna Nagar partners on Zymo — all are within 15-20 minutes of the ECR start. Best pickup for early mornings: Sholinganallur (OMR) partners.</p></section>

    <dd>East Coast Road (ECR via Thiruvanmiyur, Kovalam, Muttukadu) is the must-take route — ocean views throughout. The Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR) is shorter but cuts through IT corridor traffic with no scenic value.</dd>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does Chennai to Mahabalipuram self-drive cost?</dt>
