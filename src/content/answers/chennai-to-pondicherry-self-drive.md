    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Chennai to Pondicherry self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Chennai to Pondicherry (Puducherry) is about 150 km via the East Coast Road (ECR/NH32), approximately 3 hours. The ECR is one of India's most scenic coastal highways — 4-lane to Mahabalipuram and narrower beyond, with Bay of Bengal views, beach access at Kovalam, Mahabalipuram, and Fisherman's Cove, and the Auroville crossroads detour. All car classes handle this route; a hatchback is ideal for ECR beach-strip parking.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Chennai to Pondicherry route breakdown</h2><p>Chennai (OMR/ECR junction) → Kovalam / Fisherman's Cove (about 40 km): beach access point. → Mahabalipuram (55 km, 1 hr): Shore Temple, 5 Rathas, and Arjuna's Penance carvings. Mahabalipuram → Auroville turnoff (about 135 km from Chennai, 2.5 hrs): detour 10 km inland for the Matrimandir. Auroville → Pondicherry (13 km, 20 mins): French Quarter, White Town, Promenade Beach. Alternative inland route via GST Road and Tindivanam: about 175 km — longer, and skips the ECR scenery.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Chennai-Pondicherry self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,200-1,700/day. 2-day trip: ₹2,400-3,400. Fuel: about 300 km round trip; a hatchback at 16 km/L and ₹101/L = about ₹1,894. Tolls: ECR has toll plazas, paid via FASTag — check current rates before you leave. Mahabalipuram entry: ₹40 per person (ASI monument ticket). Auroville Matrimandir pass: free but advance online registration required. Pondicherry accommodation: ₹2,000-5,000/night at White Town heritage properties. Total 2-day budget for 2 people with one night in Pondicherry: about ₹6,374-10,374, plus tolls.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to see on the Chennai-Pondicherry ECR drive</h2><p>Mahabalipuram (55 km): UNESCO World Heritage Site — Pallava dynasty rock-cut temples. Allow 2-3 hours for a comfortable visit, including Shore Temple (sea-facing, sunset is spectacular). Crocodile Bank (45 km from Chennai): India's first crocodile conservation centre — 45-minute visit. Auroville (about 135 km): Sri Aurobindo's planned township — pass at the visitor centre for the Matrimandir viewing pass. Pondicherry French Quarter: Boulevard Dumas along the promenade, Eglise de Notre Dame des Anges, Café de Flore, and Alliance Française. Rock Beach: dawn walks are magical — avoid Sunday afternoons (very crowded).</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Sholinganallur-Siruseri (15 km from Chennai, before ECR proper): fill up at this IT corridor cluster before the coastal road begins — ECR has fewer major fuel stations than NH highways. Mahabalipuram (55 km): BPCL station near the tourist area — good stop after the monument visit. Beyond Mahabalipuram, fuel stations are sparser until Pondicherry — top up before you leave Mahabalipuram. Road conditions: ECR from Chennai to Mahabalipuram is excellent 4-lane coastal highway with sea views on the right. Mahabalipuram to Pondicherry narrows to 2-lane in sections near Kalpakkam — road is in good condition, drive at 60-70 km/h. Speed cameras near Mahabalipuram and Kalpakkam nuclear zone — maintain speed limits.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Chennai-Pondicherry ECR on Zymo</h2><p>Hatchback (i20, Swift, WagonR): best car for ECR — easy to park at beach access points, fuel-efficient on the flat coastal road, comfortable for 3-4 people on a 150 km route. Sedan (Dzire, Aura): slight comfort upgrade if driving 4 adults. Compact SUV: not needed on the ECR — road is flat and parking at Mahabalipuram's narrow temple-area lanes is harder in a larger vehicle. Only upgrade to an SUV if you also plan a hill drive such as Yelagiri or the Javadi hills. Book from OMR, Velachery, or Anna Nagar partners on Zymo for the most convenient Chennai-departure inventory.</p></section>
