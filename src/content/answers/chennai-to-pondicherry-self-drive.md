    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Chennai to Pondicherry (Puducherry) is 155 km via the East Coast Road (ECR/NH32), approximately 3 hours. The ECR is one of India's most scenic coastal highways — a 4-lane road with Bay of Bengal views, beach access at Kovalam, Mahabalipuram, and Fisherman's Cove, and the Auroville crossroads detour. All car classes handle this route; a hatchback is ideal for ECR beach-strip parking.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Chennai to Pondicherry route breakdown</h2><p>Chennai (OMR/ECR junction) → Mahabalipuram (55 km, 1 hr): first pitstop — Shore Temple, 5 Rathas, and Arjuna's Penance carvings. Mahabalipuram → Fisherman's Cove / Covelong (10 km): beach access point. Mahabalipuram → Auroville turnoff (75 km from Chennai, 1.5 hrs): detour 10 km inland for the Matrimandir. Auroville → Pondicherry (13 km, 20 mins): French Quarter, White Town, Promenade Beach. Alternative inland route via NH45: 175 km, 3.5 hrs — faster but skips ECR scenery.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Chennai-Pondicherry self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,100-1,500/day. 2-day trip: ₹2,200-3,000. Fuel: 310 km round trip; petrol hatchback at 15 km/L = ₹2,170 at PPAC June 2026 prices. Tolls: ECR has toll plazas — total Chennai-Pondicherry approximately ₹180 one-way via FASTag. Mahabalipuram entry: ₹40 per person (ASI monument ticket). Auroville Matrimandir pass: free but advance online registration required. Pondicherry accommodation: ₹2,000-5,000/night at White Town heritage properties. Total 2-day budget for 2 people: ₹8,000-14,000.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to see on the Chennai-Pondicherry ECR drive</h2><p>Mahabalipuram (55 km): UNESCO World Heritage Site — Pallava dynasty rock-cut temples. Allow 2-3 hours for a comfortable visit, including Shore Temple (sea-facing, sunset is spectacular). Crocodile Bank (45 km from Chennai): India's first crocodile conservation centre — 45-minute visit. Auroville (130 km): Sri Aurobindo's planned township — pass at the visitor centre for the Matrimandir viewing pass. Pondicherry French Quarter: Boulevard Dumas along the promenade, Eglise de Notre Dame des Anges, Café de Flore, and Alliance Française. Rock Beach: dawn walks are magical — avoid Sunday afternoons (very crowded).</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Sholinganallur-Siruseri (15 km from Chennai, before ECR proper): fill up at this IT corridor cluster before the coastal road begins — ECR has fewer major fuel stations than NH highways. Mahabalipuram (55 km): BPCL station near the tourist area — good stop after the monument visit. Tindivanam bypass (120 km): larger fuel plaza with restaurants before the final Pondicherry approach. Road conditions: ECR from Chennai to Mahabalipuram is excellent 4-lane coastal highway with sea views on the right. Mahabalipuram to Pondicherry narrows to 2-lane in sections near Kalpakkam — road is in good condition, drive at 60-70 km/h. Speed cameras near Mahabalipuram and Kalpakkam nuclear zone — maintain speed limits.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Chennai-Pondicherry ECR on Zymo</h2><p>Hatchback (i20, Swift, WagonR): best car for ECR — easy to park at beach access points, fuel-efficient on the flat coastal road, comfortable for 3-4 people on a 155 km route. Sedan (Dzire, Aura): slight comfort upgrade if driving 4 adults. Compact SUV: not needed on the ECR — road is flat and parking at Mahabalipuram's narrow temple-area lanes is harder in a larger vehicle. Only upgrade to SUV if you plan to divert to Yelagiri hills or Javadi hills beyond Pondicherry. Book from OMR, Velachery, or Anna Nagar partners on Zymo for the most convenient Chennai-departure inventory.</p></section>
