    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">What happens if I damage a self-drive rental car?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Insurance and liability rules across the Zymo network</p>
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">If you damage a self-drive rental car in India, the outcome depends on insurance coverage and severity. Mandatory third-party insurance covers damage to others, not the rental car itself. Comprehensive insurance covers own-damage minus a deductible — typically ₹5,000-15,000. Without it, you're liable for full repair cost. Photograph the car at pickup, drive carefully, contact the partner immediately.</p>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What basic (mandatory) insurance covers</h2>
    <p>Every rental car in India carries third-party insurance under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988. This covers:</p>
    <ul>
    <li>Damage you cause to ANOTHER vehicle, person, or property</li>
    <li>Injury or death of third parties</li>
    <li>Some property damage to public/private property</li>
    </ul>
    <p>It does NOT cover damage to the rental car itself, theft of the car, or natural-disaster damage. For those scenarios, you need comprehensive insurance.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What comprehensive insurance covers (and the deductible)</h2>
    <p>Most rental partners include comprehensive insurance (own-damage cover, theft, fire) as standard. A few offer it only as an upgrade. Comprehensive coverage pays for:</p>
    <ul>
    <li>Damage to the rental car from accidents (your fault)</li>
    <li>Theft of the car</li>
    <li>Damage from natural disasters (floods, fire)</li>
    <li>Vandalism</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>The deductible (excess):</strong> typically ₹5,000-15,000 depending on car class. You pay the deductible; insurance covers the rest. For a full SUV with a ₹15,000 deductible, even a ₹50,000 panel repair costs you only ₹15,000 if comprehensive coverage applies.</p>
    </section>

    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Immediate steps after any incident</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Ensure safety first.</strong> Move yourself and any injured parties to a safe location. Call 100 (police) or 102 (ambulance) for serious injuries.</li>
    <li><strong>Photograph everything.</strong> The damage to your car, the other vehicle (if any), the location, road conditions, license plates. Multiple angles. These photos protect you in any dispute.</li>
    <li><strong>Contact the rental partner immediately.</strong> Use their emergency line. Don't move the car unless safety requires it — the partner may want to dispatch their own assessor.</li>
    <li><strong>File a police FIR if required.</strong> For accidents involving injury, third-party damage, or theft. The FIR is required for insurance claims.</li>
    <li><strong>Don't admit fault on the spot.</strong> Stick to facts when speaking to police or the other driver. Let insurance underwriters determine fault.</li>
    <li><strong>Don't get repairs done independently.</strong> The rental partner has authorised garages. Independent repairs may void the comprehensive coverage.</li>
    </ol>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Damage waiver upgrade — is it worth it?</h2>
    <p>Some partners offer an optional damage waiver upgrade — for an extra ₹150-500 per day, the deductible (₹5,000-15,000) is reduced or eliminated. Worth considering when:</p>
    <ul>
    <li>You're driving in unfamiliar / high-traffic cities</li>
    <li>You're a less experienced driver</li>
    <li>The trip involves narrow roads (mountain routes, old-city centres) with parking risk</li>
    <li>The booking is long (weekly+) and the cumulative cost is high</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Skip when: short city trip in familiar area, experienced driver, careful parking habits.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Pre-pickup photo checklist (your protection)</h2>
    <p>Before driving away, photograph:</p>
    <ul>
    <li>All four corners of the car (front-left, front-right, back-left, back-right)</li>
    <li>Each side and the roof</li>
    <li>Dashboard with odometer reading visible</li>
    <li>Fuel gauge level</li>
    <li>All four tyres (sidewalls visible)</li>
    <li>Interior — seats, dashboard, any existing wear or marks</li>
    <li>Any pre-existing scratches, dents, paint chips — close-up shots</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Send the photos to yourself via WhatsApp or email before driving away (timestamps prove pre-existing damage). Confirm any damage you photographed on the partner's handover form.</p>
    </section>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What does basic insurance on a rental car cover?</dt>
    <dd>Mandatory third-party insurance covers damage or injury you cause to another vehicle, person, or property — required by law under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988. It does NOT cover damage to the rental car itself. Comprehensive coverage (own-damage, theft) is optional or bundled in by the partner — check the booking page.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much will I pay for minor scratches?</dt>
    <dd>Minor scratches (panel scuffs, parking dings, small dents) typically cost ₹2,000-8,000 depending on severity and panel. The amount is deducted from your security deposit and the rest refunded. Photograph the car at pickup so you have a baseline for any pre-existing damage.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What if I'm in a major accident?</dt>
    <dd>Contact the rental partner immediately and call emergency services if injuries are involved. Comprehensive insurance (if opted in) covers most own-damage repairs minus a deductible (typically ₹5,000-15,000). Without comprehensive, you're liable for the full repair cost beyond the security deposit.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is the insurance excess negotiable?</dt>
    <dd>Insurance excess (deductible) is set by the partner's underwriter, not negotiable per booking. Some partners offer a 'damage waiver' upgrade that reduces or removes the excess for a higher booking fee. The booking page shows whether the upgrade is offered.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Should I take photos at pickup?</dt>
    <dd>Yes — always. Photograph the car from all angles (front, back, both sides, roof, dashboard, fuel gauge) at pickup, and confirm any pre-existing damage on the partner's handover form. This is your protection against being charged for damage you didn't cause.</dd>
