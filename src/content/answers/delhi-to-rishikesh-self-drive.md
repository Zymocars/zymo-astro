    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Delhi to Rishikesh self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Delhi to Rishikesh is 240 km via NH334 through Haridwar, approximately 5 hours. Rishikesh is the white-water rafting capital of India and the gateway to Uttarakhand — Char Dham yatra base, yoga ashrams, Laxman Jhula. A hatchback or sedan handles the route; Haridwar-Rishikesh final 25 km is 2-lane mountain foothills road.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Route breakdown and key milestones</h2><p>Delhi → Ghaziabad → Meerut bypass (NH334, 100 km, 1.5 hrs): 4-lane highway to Haridwar. Meerut → Haridwar (NH334, 80 km, 1.5 hrs): continues 4-lane, passes through Muzaffarnagar and Roorkee. Haridwar → Rishikesh (25 km, 45 mins): 2-lane Ganga riverside road through foothill towns. Haridwar detour: Har Ki Pauri ghat, evening Ganga Aarti (6-7 PM — best in spring and autumn). Total Delhi to Rishikesh: 240 km, 5 hours.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Delhi-Rishikesh self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,100-1,500/day. 2-day trip: ₹2,200-3,000. Fuel: 480 km round trip; hatchback at 15 km/L = ₹3,360. Tolls: Delhi-Haridwar NH334 (₹280 one-way) — round-trip ₹560. Rishikesh white-water rafting: ₹700-1,200 per person for 16 km Brahmpuri-Rishikesh grade 2-3 stretch. Bungee jumping at Mohan Chatti: ₹3,500/person (advance booking required). Total 2-day budget for 2 people: ₹12,000-18,000 including activities.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Rishikesh activities and sightseeing by self-drive</h2><p>Laxman Jhula: suspension bridge over Ganga — walk across and explore the cafes and yoga centres on the east bank. Ram Jhula: second suspension bridge, 2 km from Laxman Jhula. Triveni Ghat: dawn Ganga aarti at 6 AM. Neelkanth Mahadev Temple (32 km from Rishikesh): forest drive up to the temple. Beatles Ashram (Maharishi Mahesh Yogi Ashram): pay ₹150 entry and explore the ruins where the Beatles recorded. Camping at Shivpuri or Byasi: 12-16 km upstream from Rishikesh — book rafting+camping packages online.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Meerut (100 km from Delhi, NH334): large city with multiple fuel stations — first clean fuel+toilet stop. Roorkee (160 km): IIT Roorkee campus town, HP station on NH334. Haridwar (215 km): fill full tank in Haridwar before the final 25 km to Rishikesh — Rishikesh city has limited fuel. Road conditions: Delhi-Haridwar NH334 is 4-lane and in good condition. Haridwar-Rishikesh road is 2-lane with pilgrimage traffic — narrow through Rishikesh's Lakshman Jhula zone.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Delhi-Rishikesh on Zymo</h2><p>Swift or i20 hatchback: ideal for the Delhi-Rishikesh route — flat NH334 doesn't require an SUV, and compact cars park more easily on Rishikesh's narrow riverside lanes. Compact SUV (Creta, Nexon): warranted if extending the trip to Devprayag, Srinagar (Garhwal), or Kedarnath (mountain roads). Avoid Innova for Rishikesh town driving — very narrow roads near the ashram belt. Book from Gurgaon, Noida, or South Delhi partners on Zymo.</p></section>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What car is best for Delhi to Rishikesh?</dt>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does Delhi to Rishikesh self-drive cost?</dt>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the best season to drive Delhi to Rishikesh?</dt>
    <dd>October-June: ideal. Rishikesh is accessible year-round — mild winters, and summer is actually pleasant (24-30°C). July-September: monsoon makes the Ganga high and rafting off-limits, but Rishikesh town is accessible.</dd>
