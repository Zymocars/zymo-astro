    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Delhi to Shimla self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>

    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Delhi to Shimla is 345 km via NH44 to Chandigarh then NH5 to Shimla, approximately 7 hours total. The first 260 km to Chandigarh is fast 6-lane highway; the final 85 km from Chandigarh (Kalka) to Shimla is a mountain road. A compact SUV is recommended — Shimla's steep lanes and narrow roads are challenging in large vehicles. The famous Kalka-Shimla Toy Train is the alternative for those who prefer rail.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Delhi to Shimla route breakdown</h2><p>Delhi → Chandigarh (NH44, 260 km, 4 hrs): India's fastest stretch — 6-lane, express lanes for most of the route. Chandigarh → Kalka (NH5, 35 km, 30 mins): transitions to foothills. Kalka → Shimla (NH5, 85 km, 2.5 hrs): narrow mountain highway with hairpin bends above Solan — average speed 30-35 km/h. Shimla city: car access is restricted in the Mall Road area — park at Rivoli Bus Stand or Lakkar Bazaar parking and walk. Note: NH44 Delhi-Chandigarh has a speed camera at Karnal and Ambala — drive within limit.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Best car for Delhi-Shimla and season guide</h2><p>Car: a compact SUV (Creta, Nexon, Seltos) is ideal. Shimla's hill roads are narrow — avoid booking a full-size SUV (Fortuner, XUV700) for city driving. Hatchbacks work in dry months (March-May, September-October) but require careful gear management on the Kalka-Shimla ghat. Season: March-May: apple orchards in bloom, mild weather, pre-peak. June-August: monsoon, fog, and occasional landslides above Solan. September-October: clear blue skies, best visibility for mountain views. November-February: snow on Mall Road and Jakhu Hill — beautiful but chains may be needed.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Delhi-Shimla self-drive</h2><p>Car: compact SUV ₹2,200-3,000/day. 3-day trip: ₹6,600-9,000. Fuel: approximately 690 km round trip; petrol SUV at 11 km/L at PPAC June 2026 prices = ₹6,600. Tolls: Delhi-Chandigarh NH44 (₹300 one-way) + Kalka-Shimla road (₹80 one-way) = ₹380 one-way, ₹760 round-trip. Shimla parking: ₹100/day at Lakkar Bazaar. Cable car to Jakhu Temple: ₹150/person. Total 3-day non-refundable budget for 2 people: ₹17,000-22,000.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Panipat (95 km from Delhi, NH44): first major fuel cluster on the highway — optional top-up. Ambala bypass (220 km): good fuel availability. Chandigarh Sector 43 / Manimajra (260 km): fill full tank here — this is the last major city before mountain driving begins. Solan (310 km, NH5): Indian Oil station near Solan bus stand — critical fuel stop; Shimla fuel stations are limited and queues can form in peak season. Road conditions: Delhi-Chandigarh NH44 is 6-lane excellent. Chandigarh-Kalka NH5 is 4-lane. Kalka-Shimla is 2-lane narrow mountain road with hairpin bends — average speed 30 km/h, drive in first-second gear on steep sections.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Delhi-Shimla on Zymo</h2><p>Hyundai Creta or Tata Nexon compact SUV: strongly recommended — good brakes for Kalka-Shimla descents, ground clearance for ghat road conditions, compact body for Shimla's narrow Mall Road approach lanes. Kia Seltos: equally capable, good cabin space for 4. Avoid full-size SUVs (Fortuner, Scorpio N) for Shimla city — the Mall Road approach lane is very narrow and large-wheelbase vehicles get stuck in reversing situations. Hatchbacks (Swift, i20): capable on Kalka-Shimla in March-May and October (dry season) but avoid December-February without confirmed snow chains and partner approval. Book from Gurgaon, Noida, or South Delhi partners on Zymo.</p></section>
