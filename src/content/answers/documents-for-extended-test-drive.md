    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">What documents do I need for an Extended Test Drive?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Across the Zymo network</p>
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">For an Extended Test Drive on Zymo, you need the same documents as a normal self-drive booking — a valid Indian driving licence (full, not learner, at least one year old), a government photo ID (Aadhaar preferred), and a credit or debit card for the refundable security deposit. Aadhaar OTP verification is completed online before pickup. No dealer paperwork or buying-intent proof is required.</p>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The three core documents</h2>
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">1. Driving licence (full, non-learner)</h3>
    <p>A valid Indian driving licence for the car class you are booking — at least one year old. Bring the physical original at pickup. Learner's licences and expired licences are not accepted. Your name on the licence must match the name on the booking.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">2. Government photo ID</h3>
    <p>Aadhaar is the most widely accepted. Passport, voter ID, and PAN card also work. The ID name must match the booking and the driving licence.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">3. Payment card for security deposit</h3>
    <p>Credit or debit card under the booking name. The deposit is held — not charged — for the rental period, and refunded within 3-5 working days after return. Deposit amounts typically range from ₹3,000 to ₹15,000 depending on car class and partner.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Aadhaar OTP verification step</h2>
    <p>Every Zymo booking includes an additional Aadhaar OTP verification step you complete before pickup — via a link sent to your WhatsApp or email. Same step as a normal self-drive booking. Completing it before reaching the partner location saves 10-15 minutes at handover.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What you do not need to bring</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>No dealer paperwork.</strong> You do not need to show a quote, a booking voucher, or any proof you are planning to buy the car.</li>
    <li><strong>No loan pre-approval.</strong> The Extended Test Drive is a self-drive booking, not a financial transaction with the OEM.</li>
    <li><strong>No purchase agreement.</strong> The decision to buy comes after the Extended Test Drive, through your OEM dealer.</li>
    </ul>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">For foreign nationals</h2>
    <p>Foreign tourists and NRIs renting an Extended Test Drive car in India need an International Driving Permit (IDP) from their home country alongside the home-country licence, plus passport with valid Indian visa, plus address proof in India (hotel booking confirmation or local host's address typically works). Some partners restrict bookings to Indian citizens only — the booking page shows partner-specific eligibility.</p>
    </section>
