    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">What documents do I need to book a self-drive car in India?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">To book a self-drive car in India you need three documents: a valid Indian driving licence (non-learner, at least one year old), a government photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, or voter ID), and a credit or debit card for the refundable security deposit. Carry originals at pickup. Foreign nationals also need an International Driving Permit.</p>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Driving licence:</strong> full (non-learner) Indian licence, valid, at least one year old</li>
    <li><strong>Government photo ID:</strong> Aadhaar preferred; passport or voter ID also accepted</li>
    <li><strong>Payment card:</strong> credit or debit card for refundable security deposit (₹3,000–₹15,000 depending on car class)</li>
    <li><strong>Carry originals:</strong> physical documents at pickup — digital copies alone are typically not accepted</li>
    </ul>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The three core documents</h2>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">1. Driving licence (full, non-learner)</h3>
    <p>Indian driving licence valid for the car class you're booking. Most partners require the licence to be at least one year old — recently-issued licences may be rejected. The licence must not be expired or suspended. Bring the physical original; photocopies and DigiLocker images are accepted by some partners as a backup but not as a replacement.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">2. Government photo ID</h3>
    <p>Aadhaar is the most widely accepted. Passport, voter ID, and PAN card also work for most partners. The ID must match the name on your driving licence and the booking. If your booking name and licence name don't match (e.g., post-marriage name change), bring proof of the change (marriage certificate or affidavit).</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">3. Payment card for security deposit</h3>
    <p>Credit or debit card under the booking name. The deposit is held (not charged) for the rental period and refunded within 5-7 working days after return, provided the car is returned without damage and with the correct fuel level. Deposit amounts typically range from ₹3,000 to ₹15,000 depending on the car class. Some partners offer no-deposit or low-deposit bookings on specific cars — visible on the booking page.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Aadhaar OTP verification on Zymo bookings</h2>
    <p>Zymo bookings include an additional Aadhaar OTP verification step you complete before pickup — via a link sent to your WhatsApp or email. This isn't a separate document; it uses the Aadhaar number you've already linked to your Zymo account. Completing it before reaching the partner speeds up handover by 10-15 minutes.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">For foreign nationals</h2>
    <p>Foreign tourists and NRIs renting in India need:</p>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>International Driving Permit (IDP)</strong> from your home country — valid for India, must be carried alongside your home-country licence (the IDP alone is not enough)</li>
    <li><strong>Home-country driving licence</strong> — original, not expired</li>
    <li><strong>Passport</strong> with valid Indian visa</li>
    <li><strong>Address in India</strong> — hotel booking confirmation or local host's address proof, accepted by most partners</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Some partners restrict rentals to Indian citizens only. The booking page on Zymo shows partner-specific eligibility before payment.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What's NOT accepted</h2>
    <ul>
    <li>Learner's licence (any state)</li>
    <li>Expired driving licence</li>
    <li>International licence without IDP (for non-residents)</li>
    <li>Photocopies or digital scans alone (DigiLocker may work as backup; carry originals)</li>
    <li>Driving licence issued under a different name than the booking</li>
    </ul>
    </section>
