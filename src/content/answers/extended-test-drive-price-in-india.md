    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">What is the price of an Extended Test Drive in India?</h1>

    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">An Extended Test Drive on Zymo is priced the same as a normal self-drive rental — no separate Extended Test Drive surcharge.     <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How the price is built</h2>
    <p>Three components decide what you pay:</p>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Daily or hourly rental rate.</strong> Set by the partner, varies by car class. A hatchback costs materially less per day than a full-size SUV. Multi-day brackets (3, 5, 7 days) typically carry better per-day rates than single-day.</li>
    <li><strong>Refundable security deposit.</strong> Held during the trip, refunded within 5-7 working days after return. Typically ₹3,000 to ₹15,000 depending on car class. Some partners offer low or zero-deposit options on specific cars — visible on the booking page.</li>
    <li><strong>Fuel.</strong> You return the car at the fuel level the partner specified at pickup. You pay for fuel you use during the trip.</li>
    </ul>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">No Zymo platform fee</h2>
    <p>Zymo does not add a separate platform fee on top of the partner's price. The number you see in the booking page is the number you pay. Compare against any other booking surface to confirm.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost in context of the car-purchase decision</h2>
    <p>A typical 2-3 day Extended Test Drive on a compact SUV runs into a few thousand rupees. The car you are evaluating costs ten to twenty lakh. If the Extended Test Drive reveals that the car does not fit your life — too cramped, too thirsty, too tall for your parking, wrong rear-seat geometry for your family — the saved depreciation in year one alone covers many Extended Test Drives. The risk-reduction math is the same as paying for a building inspection before signing a flat-purchase agreement.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to find the best price for your trip</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Compare partners.</strong> The booking page shows multiple partners offering the same car for the same dates. Same model, different prices — pick the option that fits.</li>
    <li><strong>Check multi-day brackets.</strong> A 3-day booking is often priced better per day than three 1-day bookings. Many partners offer further discounts at 5 and 7 days.</li>
    <li><strong>Off-peak dates.</strong> Weekday Extended Test Drives are often cheaper than weekend ones. If your evaluation does not need a weekend trip, a Tuesday-to-Thursday window can be more efficient.</li>
    <li><strong>City variation.</strong> Pricing varies by city — Mumbai and Bangalore typically command different rates than Tier-2 cities. The booking page is the source of truth for your specific city.</li>
    </ul>
