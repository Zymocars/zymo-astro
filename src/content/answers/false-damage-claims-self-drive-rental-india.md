    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">How do I dispute a false damage claim on a self-drive rental in India?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">If a self-drive rental partner claims damage you didn't cause, contact Zymo customer care at hello@zymo.app with your booking ID + evidence (pre-pickup photos, post-return acknowledgement, drop-off signature status). Zymo opens a dispute review with the ops team and reviews the partner's evidence against yours. Typical resolution: 3-5 working days. The outcome is evidence-based — Zymo determines the resolution based on what each side documented.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Before the trip — the checklist that wins disputes</h2><p>The single strongest defense against a false damage claim is a pre-pickup photograph set that establishes the car's condition at the moment you took possession. Photograph the car from all angles — front, back, both sides, roof, all four wheels, interior dashboard, driver-seat area, and boot. Timestamp them (most phone cameras do this automatically). Note the exact odometer reading and fuel gauge level. If the partner uses a physical handover form, cross-check the pre-existing damage marked on it against what you can see — flag any discrepancies before signing. If the handover form says 'no scratches' but you can see a rear-bumper dent, note it in writing before signing (or refuse to sign until the form is amended). These photos + notes are your dispute grounds. Customers who skip this step have a much harder time contesting later charges.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">During and after the trip — what to document</h2><p>Screenshot the Zymo booking page after confirming — capture the exact rental amount, deposit hold, kilometre cap, and fuel policy so any later dispute over 'agreed terms' has a fixed reference. At return, insist on a written or WhatsApp-based partner acknowledgement of: fuel level, odometer reading, and vehicle condition. If the partner refuses to give written acknowledgement, photograph the odometer, fuel gauge, and car exterior at drop-off and message the images to yourself via email — that timestamped self-message is a valid piece of evidence in a later dispute. If a specific incident happened during the trip (a stone chip on a highway, a parking scrape someone else caused), photograph it immediately with location visible in the frame. Concurrent evidence is far more persuasive than reconstructed evidence.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The Zymo dispute-review process (what actually happens)</h2><p>Email hello@zymo.app with your booking ID as subject or first line. Attach all evidence: pre-pickup photos, post-return acknowledgement (if any), timestamped self-messages, screenshots of the booking page. Zymo customer care opens a dispute-review ticket with the ops team. The partner is asked to submit their evidence — inspection photos, damage-cost estimate, service centre invoice if applicable. Both sides' evidence is reviewed. Typical resolution: 3-5 working days from ticket open. Zymo determines the outcome based on the balance of documented evidence — documented pre-existing damage on your handover form combined with timestamped pre-pickup photos is your strongest position; genuine collision damage backed by partner-side evidence (service centre invoice, dashcam footage) is the strongest partner position. Every damage-related reply from Zymo customer care is personally reviewed and approved by the founder before send — this is not a boilerplate flow. Escalations involving suspected partner misconduct (e.g. threatened extortion, refusal to release vehicle without cash) are hard-stopped to the founder for personal handling.</p></section>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Related questions</h2>
    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How do I dispute a false damage claim on a Zymo self-drive rental?</dt>
    <dd>When a partner adds a post-trip charge, Zymo sends it to you to review, and you have 48 hours to approve or dispute it. Dispute it from the review link, then email hello@zymo.app with your booking ID, your version of what happened at drop-off, and evidence (pre-pickup photos, post-return acknowledgement from the partner, screenshots of the booking-page fare breakdown). Zymo opens a dispute review with the ops team, and a disputed charge is resolved in 3–5 working days. Outcome is evidence-based — full refund of the disputed amount if the partner cannot substantiate the claim, partial goodwill for borderline cases, or charge stands if partner evidence is clear.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What evidence do I need to fight a false damage claim?</dt>
    <dd>Four things carry weight: (1) pre-pickup photographs of the car from all angles with timestamps, (2) the partner's own handover form with pre-existing damage marked (cross-check what's marked against what you can see), (3) written or WhatsApp-based partner acknowledgement of the car's condition at return, (4) any concurrent evidence of specific incidents (a photograph of a stone chip taken at the moment it happened, with location visible). Reconstructed evidence after the fact is much weaker than concurrent evidence.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How long does Zymo take to resolve a damage dispute?</dt>
    <dd>A disputed charge is resolved in 3–5 working days. You have 48 hours from receiving a post-trip charge to approve or dispute it; if you do not respond in that time, the charge is approved and settled from your security deposit. Send your booking ID and evidence to hello@zymo.app when you dispute. Every damage-related reply from Zymo is personally reviewed and approved by the founder — this is a real dispute-review process, not a boilerplate template.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can a Zymo partner charge me for pre-existing scratches?</dt>
    <dd>Not if the pre-existing damage was documented on the handover form OR visible in your pre-pickup photographs. Zymo's dispute review specifically checks partner damage claims against pre-pickup evidence. Refuse to sign a handover form that undercounts existing damage — flag discrepancies in writing before you take delivery. If a partner insists on charging for damage visible in your pre-pickup photos, that's a clear-cut refund case at dispute review.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What if the Zymo partner demands cash for damage at return?</dt>
    <dd>Do not pay. This is a hard-stop escalation. Refuse the cash demand, keep possession of your pre-pickup photos and any written evidence, and email hello@zymo.app immediately with the booking ID and 'partner demanding cash — need urgent escalation' as the subject line. Cash-only damage demands with off-platform payment requests are among the red flags Zymo escalates to the founder for personal handling. Any legitimate damage charge is deducted from the security deposit through the platform, not paid in cash to the partner.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I refuse to return the car until a damage dispute is resolved?</dt>
    <dd>No — return the car on schedule and pursue the dispute after. Withholding the vehicle triggers late-return penalties and worsens your position in the dispute. Your evidence-based case is strongest when you cooperate with the return process, get the partner's post-return acknowledgement in writing, and then dispute the charge with Zymo customer care. Late-return penalty accrual can also cost more than the disputed damage amount itself.</dd>
