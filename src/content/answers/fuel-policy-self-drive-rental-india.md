    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Do I need to refill petrol in a self-drive rental car?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Fuel policies across the Zymo partner network</p>
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Most self-drive rental cars in India use a full-to-full fuel policy: receive the car with a full tank, return with a full tank, paying for whatever you use. Some partners use same-to-same: return with the level you started. A few offer prepaid fuel: pay upfront, return on empty. Exact policy is visible on the Zymo booking page before payment.</p>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The three common fuel policies</h2>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">1. Full-to-full (most common)</h3>
    <p>You receive the car with a full fuel tank and must return it with a full fuel tank. You pay for everything you use. This is the most transparent policy and the most common across Indian self-drive rentals. Refill at any fuel station near the return point — the partner verifies fuel level at handover.</p>
    <p><strong>Best for:</strong> longer trips, single-fill drivers, anyone who values transparent fuel cost accounting.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">2. Same-to-same</h3>
    <p>You receive the car with whatever fuel level it has (often quarter or half tank) and must return it with the same level. Common for hourly bookings where a full-fill at handover is impractical. The partner photographs the fuel gauge at pickup and at return for verification — return with less and you pay the difference plus a refuelling fee (typically ₹100-300).</p>
    <p><strong>Best for:</strong> short hourly trips, city errands, anyone who doesn't want a refill stop before return.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">3. Prepaid fuel</h3>
    <p>You pay for a full tank at booking and can return the car with whatever fuel is left — no refill required. The unused fuel isn't refunded. Some partners offer this as an optional add-on for outstation trips where finding the same fuel station at return is impractical.</p>
    <p><strong>Best for:</strong> outstation trips ending at the partner's hub late at night, or when fuel station hours are uncertain at return.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What if I return with more fuel than I started with?</h2>
    <p>Most partners do NOT refund the difference. Plan to return with exactly the fuel level the partner specified at handover — overfilling is a free gift to the next renter, not a refund opportunity. The exception: prepaid-fuel bookings, where overfilling is irrelevant since you've already paid the fixed amount.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Diesel vs petrol vs CNG vs electric</h2>
    <p>Most rental cars in metros are petrol. SUVs and MPVs (Innova, Scorpio N, Fortuner) are typically diesel. CNG retrofits exist on some hatchbacks in Delhi NCR — check the booking page for fuel type. Electric self-drive rentals are emerging on a few partners (limited fleet, mostly for short city trips). For EV rentals, the partner specifies whether you return charged or pay a charging fee per percentage point.</p>
    </section>
