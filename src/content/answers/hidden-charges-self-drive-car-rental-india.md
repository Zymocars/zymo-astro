    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">On Zymo, there is no platform fee added on top of the partner's price. The total shown on the booking page before payment is the total you pay for the booking. Partner-level charges — security deposit, kilometre cap, fuel policy, delivery fee where applicable — are all disclosed on the booking page before you confirm. Trip costs (fuel, tolls, parking, traffic fines) are paid separately by the driver during the trip. Damage charges only apply if damage is caused during the rental and are deducted from the security deposit with the deduction visible in the refund breakdown.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The Zymo pricing transparency commitment</h2><p>Zymo does not add a booking fee, a service fee, a convenience fee, an insurance markup, or a handover charge on top of the partner's price. The partner sets the rental rate; Zymo surfaces it as-is. The full fare breakdown appears on the booking page before you commit: rental amount (per hour or per day), security deposit amount, kilometre cap for the booked duration, extra-km rate if you exceed the cap, fuel policy (full-to-full, same-to-same, or prepaid), and any partner-specific delivery fee for doorstep pickup. If you don't see one of these on the booking page for a specific listing, message Zymo support before confirming the booking — do not assume.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What you DO pay separately (and why it's not a hidden charge)</h2><p>Some costs are inherently variable and paid during the trip rather than at booking — this is standard across every self-drive rental platform globally, not a Zymo-specific concept. Fuel: you refuel at retail petrol pumps during and at the end of the trip; Zymo does not include fuel in the booking price for full-to-full policies. Tolls: paid via FASTag (which most partner cars have pre-installed) as you cross toll plazas; the FASTag balance is either your responsibility to keep topped up or is deducted from your deposit post-trip depending on partner policy — visible on booking page. Parking: paid where required at destinations. Traffic fines and challans: your responsibility if incurred during the rental, deducted from deposit post-trip. Damage: if you cause damage, the repair cost is deducted from your deposit with the specific deduction shown in the refund breakdown you receive.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to protect yourself from surprise charges</h2><p>Read the booking page before payment. Every fee you'll be responsible for should appear there — if something isn't listed and the partner tries to charge it at pickup or return, you have grounds to refuse. Screenshot the booking page after confirming, especially the fare breakdown. If a partner adds a charge at pickup that wasn't disclosed on the booking page, decline pickup and message Zymo support with your screenshot — the platform can override the partner or refund the booking. At return, insist on a written statement of any deduction from the deposit (WhatsApp, email, or handover form). Cross-check credit card statements the day the refund lands: if the refunded amount is less than expected without a documented deduction, dispute via Zymo support with booking ID + screenshots as evidence.</p></section>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Does Zymo charge a booking fee or platform fee?</dt>
    <dd>No. Zymo does not add a booking fee, service fee, convenience fee, insurance markup, or handover charge on top of the partner's rental price. The total shown on the booking page before you pay is the total you pay for the booking itself. This is Zymo's core pricing-transparency commitment.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What charges could appear at pickup that weren't in the booking?</dt>
    <dd>On a properly-disclosed booking, none. Every partner-level charge — security deposit, kilometre cap, extra-km rate, fuel policy, delivery fee — is shown on the booking page before you pay. If a partner tries to add a fee at pickup that wasn't disclosed, you can decline pickup and message Zymo support — the platform can override the partner charge or refund the booking.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Are tolls and fuel included in the Zymo rental price?</dt>
    <dd>No, and this is not a hidden charge — it is standard across all Indian self-drive platforms. The rental price on Zymo covers the car booking only. Fuel (you refuel at retail petrol pumps), tolls (paid via FASTag as you drive), parking, and traffic fines are all paid separately by the driver during the trip. Damage costs are deducted from the security deposit only if damage is caused during the rental.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the full fare breakdown on a Zymo booking?</dt>
    <dd>The booking page shows: (1) rental amount for the hours or days booked, (2) refundable security deposit amount, (3) kilometre cap for the booked duration, (4) extra-km rate if you exceed the cap, (5) fuel policy (full-to-full, same-to-same, or prepaid), (6) partner-specific delivery fee if opting for doorstep pickup. Nothing else appears at the payment step. If a listing is missing any of these six on the booking page, message Zymo support before you confirm.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How do I dispute a surprise charge from the rental partner?</dt>
    <dd>Screenshot the booking page fare breakdown at time of booking. If a surprise charge appears at pickup, return, or on your card statement, contact Zymo support with your booking ID and the screenshot. Zymo can cross-check the partner's charge against what was disclosed at booking time and escalate any discrepancy on your behalf.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Why does the total on my card statement differ from the booking price?</dt>
    <dd>Three legitimate reasons: (1) the security deposit, paid with the booking and refunded by Zymo within 5–7 working days after the trip; (2) partner-recovered charges for fuel shortfall, tolls, traffic fines, or damage incurred during the trip — deducted from deposit and shown in the refund breakdown; (3) a currency conversion charge if you paid with an international card. Any other discrepancy is disputable via Zymo support.</dd>
