    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Is a self-drive car rental good for a hill-station trip in India?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Yes, with the right car class. Hill-station drives (Manali, Shimla, Ooty, Munnar, Coorg, Nainital, Mahabaleshwar, Kodaikanal) reward SUVs with ground clearance over the broken ghat sections and the rougher final-mile roads. Hatchbacks and sedans handle the standard tourist routes (Bangalore-Coorg via the new SH88, Delhi-Shimla, Cochin-Munnar) during dry season but should be avoided in monsoon.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Class fit by hill station</h2><p>Shimla/Kodaikanal/Mahabaleshwar — sedan or SUV. Ooty (Nilgiris hairpins) — hatchback or SUV both work; Sigur Ghat's 36 bends are easier in a smaller car. Manali — SUV strongly preferred, mandatory in monsoon and December-February snow. Coorg/Munnar — sedan for paved tourist routes, SUV for estate-stay access roads. Nainital — sedan for Nainital town, SUV for Bhimtal/Mukteshwar onward.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Monsoon and winter caveats</h2><p>June-September monsoon — landslide debris is common on every major hill route. SUVs with higher ground clearance handle this; sedans struggle. December-February — Manali, Shimla, Mussoorie, and high-altitude Nainital approaches need snow-chain capability — confirm with the partner that the car is winterised. Cochin-Munnar and the South Indian hill stations don't see snow but get heavy monsoon flooding.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Booking lead time</h2><p>Hill-station SUV bookings for long weekends (Diwali week, Christmas, New Year, May summer vacation) book out 10-14 days ahead. Book early or accept a higher daily rate for short-notice availability. Smaller hatchbacks are easier to find on short notice but offer less hill-driving comfort.</p></section>
    
    
    
    <section id="faq">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Related questions</h2>
    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Which car class is best for Manali?</dt>
    <dd>SUV. Manali drives via NH3 hit steep gradients past Mandi, broken sections through Pandoh-Aut, and snow-chain capability in December-February. Ground clearance (190-210mm) and traction matter. Creta, Seltos, Scorpio, XUV700 are popular.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I drive a hatchback to Shimla?</dt>
    <dd>Yes, in dry season (October-November). The Kalka-Shimla road has tight bends a smaller car negotiates easily. Avoid monsoon (July-September landslide debris) and December-February peak snow. Sedans and compact SUVs are alternatives for more comfort.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is a self-drive safe for Ooty in monsoon?</dt>
    <dd>An SUV is materially safer. The Sigur Ghat's 36 hairpins and Bandipur's broken sections become aggressive in monsoon; SUV ground clearance and traction handle this better than a sedan or hatchback. Plan a dry-season alternative if monsoon makes you nervous.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What documents do I need for an inter-state hill trip?</dt>
    <dd>Same as a normal self-drive: Indian driving licence, government photo ID, payment card for deposit. Some Northeast and J&K crossings have additional inner-line permits — confirm with the partner at booking.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How early should I book a hill-station SUV?</dt>
    <dd>10-14 days ahead for long-weekend windows (Diwali, Christmas, New Year, May summer vacation, August Independence Day). Hatchback availability holds up shorter-notice; SUV inventory tightens fast.</dd>
