    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">How AI is changing car rental in India — a first-person operator's view</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">By <a href="/founder" style="color:#4F7BFF;text-decoration:none">Manish Pratik</a>, Founder of Zymo &middot; June 2026</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">I founded Zymo — a self-drive car rental aggregator across 60+ Indian cities, comparing cars from multiple partners. This is an operator's view of where AI helps a car rental business today and where it does not — not the conference-talk version.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What AI is actually replacing in car rental operations</h2><p>AI takes on the repetitive execution layer: SEO content generation and optimisation, email drafts and outreach, data analysis, performance monitoring and alerts. It does not replace: product decisions, partnership negotiations, platform positioning, handling complex customer escalations, or understanding what Tier-2 city buyers actually want. The mistake most operators make is deploying AI for the second category and expecting the same output.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Where people still decide</h2><p>Pricing a car, choosing which cities to serve, agreeing terms with a rental partner and resolving a customer escalation all need judgement and context that AI does not have. AI can draft, summarise and monitor; a person reviews the output and makes the decision.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What this means for the car rental industry</h2><p>AI eliminates the need for large headcount in the marketing, content, SEO, and fundraising functions. A small team running a 60+ city operation is now possible because AI absorbs the coordination and execution load. For car rental specifically: AI-driven SEO for city and car pages, automated partner communications, programmatic pricing analysis, and demand forecasting are the near-term wins. The operators who deploy AI for execution now and keep humans for strategy and relationships will win the next 5 years.</p></section>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can AI replace human operators in car rental?</dt>
    <dd>No. AI replaces execution-layer repetitive work — writing, analysis, monitoring, outreach. It does not replace strategic judgment on market positioning, partnership relationships, or operational decisions requiring contextual understanding. The right model: AI runs the execution layer, humans direct strategy.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is an AI operating system for a business?</dt>
    <dd>A set of AI tools, each with a defined scope and its own records, that handle repeatable work — rather than a single chatbot. A person sets direction, reviews the output and makes the decisions.</dd>
