    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">How does self-drive car rental work in India?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Step-by-step guide to booking, pickup, driving, and return</p>
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Self-drive car rental in India works in five steps: search cars by city and dates, compare options across rental partners, book and pay online, complete identity verification, then pick up the car (or get doorstep delivery). You drive yourself, return at the same point with the same fuel level, and get your security deposit back in 5-7 days.</p>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The five steps in detail</h2>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Step 1 — Search</h3>
    <p>Enter your city, pickup date and time, return date and time on zymo.app. Filter by car class (hatchback, sedan, SUV, MPV) or specific model. The platform compares prices across multiple rental partners — same Hyundai Creta in Mumbai shows up multiple times from different partners with different prices, pickup points, and policies on one screen.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Step 2 — Compare</h3>
    <p>For each car, the booking page shows: rental price, security deposit, kilometre cap per day, extra-km charge, fuel policy, pickup model (doorstep / hub / self-service), partner name, and partner-specific policies. Pick the option that fits your trip and budget.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Step 3 — Book and pay</h3>
    <p>Pay securely via Cashfree (cards, UPI, net banking). Zymo charges zero platform fee — the rental price is the partner's price. The total appears before payment so there are no surprises. You get an instant booking confirmation with the partner's contact and pickup details.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Step 4 — Verify and pick up</h3>
    <p>Complete the Aadhaar OTP verification step (a link sent to your WhatsApp or email). Pick up the car from the partner's hub or designated zone, OR receive it at your address (if doorstep delivery is enabled). Photograph the car at handover, sign the partner's handover form, collect the keys.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Step 5 — Drive and return</h3>
    <p>Drive normally. Refuel as needed (most partners use full-to-full or same-to-same fuel policies). On return, the partner inspects the car, verifies fuel level, and processes the security deposit refund (typically credited to your original payment method within 5-7 working days).</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What's included in the rental price</h2>
    <ul>
    <li>Vehicle rental for the booked duration</li>
    <li>Mandatory third-party insurance (legal requirement)</li>
    <li>Comprehensive insurance (with deductible) — included by most partners, optional add-on for some</li>
    <li>Daily kilometre cap (typically 100-300 km/day depending on partner; unlimited on some monthly plans)</li>
    </ul>
    <p style="margin-top:1rem"><strong style="color:#FFFFFF">Not included (paid separately):</strong> fuel, tolls, parking fees, traffic fines, extra-km charges beyond the cap, damage waiver upgrade (optional with some partners).</p>
    </section>
