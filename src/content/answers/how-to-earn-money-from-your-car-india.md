    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">You can earn from your personal car in India by listing it on a self-drive rental aggregator like Zymo. A car booked 12-15 days a month earns an estimated ₹10,000-40,000 per month depending on model and city (an estimate, not a promise). Zymo partners keep 75 percent of the booking amount and Zymo's commission is 25 percent; it covers listing, customer acquisition, payment gateway, document checks, and support. Payouts are weekly, every Monday, by bank transfer for trips completed till the previous Friday. Putting your car to work on the days you don't need it turns a depreciating asset into monthly income.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How the earnings math actually works</h2><p>A compact SUV like Hyundai Creta lists at about ₹2,200 per day. At 12-15 booked days a month that is ₹26,400-33,000 gross, and the 75 percent owner share is ₹19,800-24,750 per month before your own fuel, insurance and wear. A hatchback like Swift lists at about ₹1,200 per day; 12-15 days = ₹14,400-18,000 gross, ₹10,800-13,500 owner share. Larger cars list at higher daily rates, so a Creta, XUV700, or Innova earns more than a hatchback for the same booked days. Utilisation matters more than daily rate — a car booked only 5 days per month earns a third of a car booked 15 days per month, regardless of class. Booked days vary by city, season and car class.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to list your car on Zymo</h2><p>Visit zymo.app/earn-from-your-car or the Partner section on the app. Submit basic details — car model, year, city, pickup neighbourhood, KYC, RC book, any valid insurance policy, and Pollution Under Control certificate. Zymo's partner-onboarding team verifies documents, sends a physical inspection form for the car's current condition (existing damage photographed at intake so it is not billed to you later), and approval usually completes within a few working days. You control availability calendar, pickup neighbourhood, and daily rate (Zymo suggests market-competitive pricing based on live comparable listings for your model and city). Bookings arrive via the partner app; you hand the car to the customer at the agreed time. Payouts are weekly, every Monday, by bank transfer for trips completed till the previous Friday.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What Zymo handles vs what you handle</h2><p>Zymo handles: customer acquisition, booking payment collection, security deposit hold, Aadhaar OTP verification of every renter, damage-claim workflow, customer support, and a weekly payout to your bank every Monday (trips completed till the previous Friday). You handle: keeping the car clean and mechanically ready, coordinating pickup and return with the customer at your convenience, informing Zymo of any long-term unavailability (service or family use), and keeping a valid insurance policy on the car. There is no exclusivity — you can list on Zymo alone or alongside other platforms.</p></section>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How to rent my car in India without a driver?</dt>

Customers drive the car themselves after Aadhaar OTP verification and security deposit hold. You do not need to be present during the trip — the customer picks up and returns to your designated pickup point.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How to give my car on rent to Uber or Ola?</dt>
    <dd>Uber and Ola require a commercial vehicle registration and yellow number plate — most private cars are not eligible. Self-drive rental via Zymo works with your existing white-plate private registration, which is simpler and less regulatory-heavy.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I give my car on rent to the government?</dt>
    <dd>Government leasing typically requires a commercial vehicle registration and a fleet-operator agreement — hard for a single private owner. The realistic private-owner path is peer-to-peer self-drive rental via aggregators like Zymo, or ride-hail leasing after converting to commercial plates. Zymo does not require plate conversion.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How to earn money from my private car in India?</dt>
    <dd>Two viable paths for a white-plate private car: list on Zymo for self-drive rentals (no plate conversion needed, and the aggregator handles customer-facing work), or convert to yellow-plate commercial and drive for Uber or Ola yourself. Passive income favours the first option; full-time income favours the second.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How to maximize car rental earnings on Zymo?</dt>
    <dd>Three levers: (1) list in a metro or Tier-1 city where daily rate and utilisation are both higher, (2) keep the car available across weekends and holidays (peak demand), (3) maintain the car well and respond quickly to customer messages — good customer ratings make renters more likely to pick your car. Owners who follow these three consistently tend to earn more than partners who list once and then stay passive.</dd>
