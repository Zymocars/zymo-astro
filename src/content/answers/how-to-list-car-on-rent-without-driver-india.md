Your job at handover is the car, not the driving: meet the customer at the agreed pickup point, complete the handover form documenting the car's condition, fuel level, and odometer reading, and take the car back at return. The car must carry a valid insurance policy throughout. You set your availability calendar, pickup neighbourhood, and daily rate — Zymo suggests market-competitive pricing based on live comparable listings for your model and city.</p></section>

List via zymo.app/earn-from-your-car or the Partner section of the app. Zymo's partner-onboarding team verifies documents, sends an inspection form for the car's current condition (pre-existing damage is photographed at intake so it is not billed to you later), and approval usually completes within a few working days. There is no exclusivity — you can list on Zymo alone or alongside other platforms.</p></section>

You block dates whenever you need the car yourself.</p></section>
