    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Hyderabad to Tirupati self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Hyderabad to Tirupati is 565 km via NH65 to Kurnool then NH67 to Tirupati, approximately 9 hours. Tirupati is home to Sri Venkateswara Temple at Tirumala — the world's most-visited pilgrimage site with over 50,000 daily visitors. Tirumala darshan tickets must be booked online before the trip. An SUV is preferred for the Tirumala ghat climb.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Route breakdown and milestones</h2><p>Hyderabad → Kurnool via NH65 (215 km, 3.5 hrs): 4-lane national highway through Nalgonda and Miryalguda. Kurnool → Kadapa via NH40 (135 km, 2.5 hrs): 4-lane road. Kadapa → Tirupati via NH67 (215 km, 3 hrs): state highway, 2-4 lane, passes through Cuddapah and Chittoor. Total: 565 km, 9 hours. Most visitors stop overnight in Tirupati and take the free shuttle bus or ghat road to Tirumala the next morning. Alternative: Hyderabad-Tirupati via Vijayawada-Nellore (620 km, 10 hrs) — longer but easier 4-lane highway all the way.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Hyderabad-Tirupati self-drive</h2><p>Car: compact SUV ₹2,200-3,000/day × 2 days = ₹4,400-6,000. Fuel: 1,130 km round trip; petrol SUV at 11 km/L = ₹10,800 — budget ₹11,000. Tolls: NH65 Hyderabad-Kurnool (₹180), NH40 Kurnool-Kadapa (₹120), NH67 Kadapa-Tirupati (₹100) = ₹400 one-way, ₹800 round-trip. Tirupati-Tirumala ghat road toll: ₹300 per car. Total non-refundable 2-day budget for 2 people: approximately ₹20,000-26,000.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Tirumala darshan planning (mandatory reading)</h2><p>Sri Venkateswara Temple online darshan tickets: book at tirupatibalaji.ap.gov.in 60-90 days in advance for Sarvadarshanam slots (₹300 per person) or Sudarshana darshan (₹1,500 — 3-4 hour wait maximum). Walk-in Sarvadarshanam queues routinely reach 8-12 hours. Tirumala ghat road: 24 km from Tirupati town — two-lane, 18 sharp hairpin bends, manageable in compact SUV or hatchback. Alternative: Tirupati-Tirumala free APSRTC bus (no car required — bus every 20 minutes). Car parking on Tirumala: limited; pre-book via TTD online parking portal.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Kurnool (215 km from Hyderabad): large city, fill full tank here before the Kadapa route. Kadapa (350 km): second fuel stop — BPCL and HPCL stations on NH67 approach. Chittoor (480 km): last major fuel town before Tirupati. Road conditions: NH65 Hyderabad-Kurnool is 4-lane good. NH40 Kurnool-Kadapa is 4-lane reasonable. NH67 Kadapa-Tirupati is 2-4 lane, some poor-surface sections near Chittoor — drive at 60-70 km/h. Tirupati ghat road (18 hairpins): narrow, well-maintained, heavily supervised.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Hyderabad-Tirupati on Zymo</h2><p>Hyundai Creta or Kia Seltos: recommended for this long 9-hour drive plus ghat climbs. Comfortable rear seat for tired pilgrims, good fuel economy on the flat NH65. Hatchback (Swift, Baleno): manages the ghat road comfortably and is more fuel-efficient on the highway — good choice for 2 people. Innova Crysta: ideal for a family pilgrimage of 5-6 but the Tirumala ghat is narrow — experienced driver required. Book from Banjara Hills or Hitech City Zymo partners for the most convenient Hyderabad-side departure.</p></section>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Related questions</h2>
    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How far is Hyderabad to Tirupati by self-drive?</dt>
    <dd>Hyderabad to Tirupati is 550 km and takes 9-10 hours via NH65 through Kurnool and Nandyal, or 9 hours via NH44 through Kurnool. The road is mostly 4-lane NH until the final ghat section to Tirumala.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Which route is best from Hyderabad to Tirupati?</dt>
    <dd>Via NH44 → NH167 through Kurnool and Nandyal to Tirupati is the standard route. Via Ongole on the coast (NH16) is similar distance but avoids the ghat section entirely if you're staying in Tirupati town rather than Tirumala.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I drive to Tirumala temple by self-drive?</dt>
    <dd>You can drive to Tirupati town freely. For Tirumala Hill (9 km of ghat road), private vehicles need a special darshan slot that includes vehicle entry — book through TTD's official website. General entry: only TTD buses permitted.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What car is best for Hyderabad to Tirupati?</dt>
    <dd>Compact SUV (Creta, Nexon) or mid-size SUV for the Tirumala ghat road — 9 km of sharp turns. If only visiting Tirupati town (SV temple, Kapila Theertham), any car works.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does Hyderabad to Tirupati self-drive cost?</dt>
    <dd>Approximately ₹15,000-22,000 for 2 days: car ₹5,000-8,000 (SUV, 2 days) + fuel ₹8,000-10,000 (550 km × 2 × ₹9/km) + tolls ₹600 + TTD accommodation + darshan slot.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I book a Hyderabad self-drive car for Tirupati?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Zymo Hyderabad partners with Andhra Pradesh outstation cover the Tirupati route. Book a 2-day package (1,200 km cap). Confirm AP outstation at booking.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the best time to visit Tirupati by self-drive?</dt>
    <dd>Avoid peak festival periods (Brahmotsavam, September; Vaikuntha Dwadasi, December-January) unless you have pre-booked darshan slots. Regular days: weekday mornings are better. Budget 3-4 hours waiting time even with a slot.</dd>
