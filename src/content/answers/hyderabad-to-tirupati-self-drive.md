    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Hyderabad to Tirupati self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Hyderabad to Tirupati is about 550 km via NH44 to Kurnool, then via Kadapa on NH40 and NH716, approximately 9 hours. Tirupati is home to Sri Venkateswara Temple at Tirumala, one of India's most visited pilgrimage sites. Tirumala darshan tickets must be booked online before the trip. An SUV is preferred for the Tirumala ghat climb.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Route breakdown and milestones</h2><p>Hyderabad → Kurnool via NH44 (215 km, 3.5 hrs): 4-lane national highway through Jadcherla. Kurnool → Kadapa via NH40 (about 190 km, 3 hrs). Kadapa → Tirupati via NH716 (about 140 km, 2.5 hrs): 2-4 lane, passes Rajampet and Renigunta. Total: about 550 km, 9 hours. Most visitors stop overnight in Tirupati and take the free shuttle bus or ghat road to Tirumala the next morning. Alternative: Hyderabad-Tirupati via Vijayawada-Nellore (620 km, 10 hrs) — longer but easier 4-lane highway all the way.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Hyderabad-Tirupati self-drive</h2><p>Car: compact SUV ₹2,200-3,000/day × 2 days = ₹4,400-6,000. Fuel: about 1,100 km round trip; petrol SUV at 11 km/L = about ₹10,500. Tolls: tolls vary by route and vehicle class and are paid via FASTag — check current rates in your FASTag or NHAI app before you leave, and the Tirumala ghat road has its own vehicle fee. Car and fuel for 2 days: the car plus about ₹10,500 of fuel, plus tolls and the Tirumala ghat fee.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Tirumala darshan planning (mandatory reading)</h2><p>Sri Venkateswara Temple darshan: book Special Entry Darshan (₹300 per person) on the official TTD portal well in advance; the free Sarva Darshan queue can run for many hours. Tirumala ghat road: about 20 km from Tirupati town — two-lane with sharp hairpin bends, manageable in a compact SUV or hatchback. Alternative: APSRTC buses run frequently between Tirupati and Tirumala (no car required). Car parking on Tirumala is limited.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Kurnool (215 km from Hyderabad): large city, fill full tank here before the Kadapa route. Kadapa (about 405 km): second fuel stop. Renigunta, just before Tirupati: fuel available. Road conditions: NH44 Hyderabad-Kurnool is 4-lane good. NH40 Kurnool-Kadapa is reasonable. NH716 Kadapa-Tirupati is 2-4 lane — drive at 60-70 km/h. Tirumala ghat road: narrow hairpins, well-maintained, heavily supervised.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Hyderabad-Tirupati on Zymo</h2><p>Hyundai Creta or Kia Seltos: recommended for this long 9-hour drive plus ghat climbs. Comfortable rear seat for tired pilgrims, good fuel economy on the flat NH44. Hatchback (Swift, Baleno): manages the ghat road comfortably and is more fuel-efficient on the highway — good choice for 2 people. Innova Crysta: ideal for a family pilgrimage of 5-6 but the Tirumala ghat is narrow — experienced driver required. Book from Banjara Hills or Hitech City Zymo partners for the most convenient Hyderabad-side departure.</p></section>

    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How far is Hyderabad to Tirupati by self-drive?</dt>
    <dd>Hyderabad to Tirupati is about 550 km and takes about 9 hours via Kurnool. The road is mostly 4-lane NH until the final ghat section to Tirumala.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Which route is best from Hyderabad to Tirupati?</dt>
    <dd>NH44 to Kurnool, then NH40 through Nandyal and Kadapa, and NH716 to Tirupati is the standard route. The route via Vijayawada and Nellore on NH16 is longer (about 620 km) but 4-lane most of the way.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I drive to Tirumala temple by self-drive?</dt>
    <dd>You can drive to Tirupati town and up the Tirumala ghat road (about 20 km) in a private car; vehicles are checked at the Alipiri gate. Darshan needs a separate booking on TTD's official website.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What car is best for Hyderabad to Tirupati?</dt>
    <dd>Compact SUV (Creta, Nexon) or mid-size SUV for the Tirumala ghat road — about 20 km of sharp turns. If only visiting Tirupati town (Kapila Theertham, Govindaraja Swamy Temple), any car works.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does Hyderabad to Tirupati self-drive cost?</dt>
    <dd>For car and fuel over 2 days: car ₹4,400-6,000 (compact SUV) + fuel about ₹10,500 (1,100 km round trip at 11 km/L and ₹105/L), plus tolls, TTD accommodation and darshan.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I book a Hyderabad self-drive car for Tirupati?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Zymo Hyderabad partners with Andhra Pradesh outstation cover the Tirupati route. Book a 2-day package (1,200 km cap). Confirm AP outstation at booking.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the best time to visit Tirupati by self-drive?</dt>
    <dd>Avoid peak festival periods (Brahmotsavam, September; Vaikuntha Ekadasi, December-January) unless you have pre-booked darshan slots. Regular days: weekday mornings are better. Budget 3-4 hours waiting time even with a slot.</dd>
