    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Kochi to Munnar self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Kochi to Munnar is 120 km via NH85 (Ernakulam-Munnar road), approximately 3.5 hours including the ghat section. Munnar is Kerala's most popular hill station at 1,600m — tea estates, Mattupetty Dam, Eravikulam National Park (Nilgiri Tahr). A compact SUV is recommended for the ghat road; a hatchback manages in dry season. Budget: car ₹2,200-2,800/day + fuel ₹2,100 round trip.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Kochi to Munnar route breakdown</h2><p>Kochi (Edapally) → Kothamangalam via NH85 (55 km, 1 hr): flat road through Perumbavoor and Aluva. Kothamangalam → Adimali via NH85 (35 km, 1 hr): road begins climbing, enters Ernakulam-Idukki forest boundary. Adimali → Munnar (30 km, 1 hr): ghat section, narrowing roads through Rajamala reserve forest. Total: 120 km, 3.5 hours. From Edapally, not Fort Kochi — Fort Kochi to Edapally adds 30 minutes in city traffic. Depart Kochi before 7 AM for best visibility on the ghat and to beat tourist traffic on Rajamala road.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Kochi-Munnar self-drive</h2><p>Car: compact SUV ₹2,200-2,800/day. 2-day trip: ₹4,400-5,600. Fuel: 240 km round trip; petrol SUV at 11 km/L at ₹105/L = ₹2,290 — budget ₹2,300. Tolls: NH85 has one toll plaza near Kothamangalam (₹55 one-way, ₹110 round-trip). Eravikulam National Park entry: ₹200 per person + bus fee ₹55 (no private vehicle entry to park). Mattupetty Dam boating: ₹65 per person. Total 2-day budget for 2 people: ₹12,000-18,000 including hotel.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Munnar activities and driving circuit</h2><p>Eravikulam National Park (15 km from Munnar): Nilgiri Tahr conservation area — entry by park bus only; private cars not allowed inside. Book entry online at eravikulam.org well in advance in season (January-April). Mattupetty Dam (12 km from Munnar): boating and High Range views. Top Station (32 km from Munnar): highest motorable point in Munnar at 1,700m, views of Rajamala. Tea Museum (2 km from Munnar town): Tata Tea processing and history. Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary (60 km): elephant and Indian Star Tortoise reserve.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Perumbavoor (30 km from Kochi): first fuel stop on NH85. Kothamangalam (55 km): good fuel and food stop before the ghat begins. Adimali (90 km): last major fuel station before the Munnar ghat — fill full tank here. Munnar town: petrol stations available near the bus stand. Road conditions: Kochi-Kothamangalam is flat 4-lane good. Kothamangalam-Munnar becomes 2-lane ghat road with tight corners after Adimali — drive at 40-50 km/h. Fog December-February on Rajamala section — switch on headlights and reduce speed.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Kochi-Munnar on Zymo</h2><p>Tata Nexon or Hyundai Creta: top picks — ground clearance for the Adimali-Munnar ghat section and compact body for Munnar's narrow town roads. Swift hatchback: manages in October-April dry season on the main ghat road; avoid in monsoon (June-August) where ghat road gets slippery in places. Innova Crysta: too wide for some of Munnar's narrower mountain roads — discouraged unless you're a very experienced hill driver. Book from Edapally or Kakkanad Zymo partners for the most convenient Kochi-side departure close to NH85.</p></section>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Related questions</h2>
    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How far is Kochi to Munnar by self-drive?</dt>
    <dd>Kochi to Munnar is 130 km and takes 3.5-4 hours via Muvattupuzha and Kothamangalam. The ghat section begins at Neriamangalam (70 km from Kochi) — the final 60 km is a winding mountain road.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Which route is best from Kochi to Munnar?</dt>
    <dd>Via NH544E (Kothamangalam → Neriamangalam → Adimali → Munnar): 130 km, the standard and most direct route. Via Thekkady is longer (200 km) but passes through Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary — good if adding Thekkady to the trip.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What car is best for Kochi to Munnar?</dt>
    <dd>Compact SUV (Creta, Nexon, Seltos) is strongly recommended. The Neriamangalam-Munnar ghat has 60 km of tight switchbacks and steep gradients. Automatic SUVs with hill start assist are safest.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the best time for Kochi to Munnar self-drive?</dt>
    <dd>September-March: post-monsoon to pre-summer, ideal weather. January: peak tea bloom season — stunning scenery. April-May: warm, fewer crowds. June-August: heavy monsoon — ghat roads can be misty and risky; waterfalls spectacular.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does Kochi to Munnar self-drive cost?</dt>
    <dd>Approximately ₹12,000-18,000 for 2 days: Kochi car rental ₹5,000-8,000 (SUV, 2 days) + fuel ₹2,500-3,500 (130 km × 2 + local driving) + Munnar accommodation ₹3,000-8,000.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I book a Kochi self-drive car for Munnar?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Zymo Kochi partners allow Kerala outstation covering the Munnar route. Book a compact SUV and confirm Kerala outstation at booking. Neriamangalam toll is ₹40.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What should I see on the Kochi-Munnar route?</dt>
    <dd>Bhoothathankettu Dam (40 km from Kochi), Neriamangalam Bridge, Valara and Cheeyappara Waterfalls (on the ghat road), Eravikulam National Park in Munnar (Nilgiri Tahr sightings), Mattupetty Dam, and Top Station viewpoint (32 km from Munnar).</dd>
