    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">What is the late return penalty on a self-drive car rental in India?</h1>

    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Late return penalties on self-drive rentals in India are set by each rental partner, not by the aggregator. The partner sets the late-return and extension charges, and the booking page shows them before you pay. A long delay can be charged as an extra day. Repeated late returns can result in the partner blacklisting your account. There is no automatic grace period documented — return by the exact time on your booking page, or request an extension in advance. Extensions requested before your original return time are typically free of penalty if the car is not already booked for the next slot.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How the late-return penalty scale actually works</h2><p>The partner sets the late-return and extension charges, and the booking page shows them before you pay. Many partners charge by the hour for a short delay and switch to a full-day charge for a long one, because a long delay can make the car miss the next customer's booking. If the late-return charge is not visible on the booking page, message Zymo customer care before confirming the booking. Penalty amounts come out of your security deposit; if the deposit does not cover the accrued penalty, Zymo collects the difference through your booking. Do not pay the partner in cash.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to avoid late-return penalties (extension request flow)</h2><p>The single most effective preventive: request an extension BEFORE your original return time. Extensions requested in advance — via the Zymo app, booking page, or partner support — are typically free of penalty if the car is not already booked for the next slot. The extra hours or days are charged at the partner's extension rate, shown in the app before you confirm. If the car is already booked for the next slot, the partner cannot grant an extension and you must return on time. Ask at least 12-24 hours before your scheduled return for maximum flexibility; 2-4 hours ahead is a fallback but risks the partner already committing the car. Do not wait until you are already past the return time to request an extension — post-return-time extensions are subject to standard late-penalty rates because the partner is already contractually in a late-return position.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to do if you know you will be late</h2><p>Message the partner directly on their contact number (available on your booking receipt) as soon as you realise you'll be late. A partner-side heads-up reduces friction even if you don't get a formal extension — some partners are able to bump the next customer by 30-60 minutes to accommodate you, especially if the next booking is a partner-flexible slot. If the partner is unreachable, message Zymo customer care at hello@zymo.app with your booking ID and 'running late, need extension' as the subject line. Do not simply return late without any communication — the partner treats silent late returns as no-communication penalties, which typically incur the full late-return rate plus a small no-notice surcharge on some partners. Do not attempt to negotiate the penalty at return time; partner staff at handover cannot waive fees. If you believe a specific late-return charge was applied unfairly (e.g. you notified the partner in advance and got a verbal OK), dispute it via Zymo customer care with your booking ID + the message thread — Zymo can review the partner's records against your evidence and adjust where warranted.</p></section>
