    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Why can the rental age be higher than the licence age?</h2>
    <p>India's driving licence age is 18, but many rental partners set higher minimums because their insurance underwriters base premiums on driving experience and accident statistics. Drivers under 21 carry meaningfully higher accident frequency and insurance premiums. Higher-value vehicles (SUVs, luxury cars) carry larger repair bills, so partners apply older minimums to manage risk.</p>
    <p>Rental companies outside India also set minimum ages above the licence age for the same insurance reasons — it is not an India-specific quirk.</p>
    </section>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Young driver surcharges</h2>
    <p>A few partners apply a "young driver" surcharge for younger drivers on top of the regular rate. Most partners don't apply this surcharge. The booking page on Zymo shows the partner's policy and any applicable surcharges before payment.</p>

    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Maximum age limit?</h2>
    <p>Most partners have no hard upper age limit. A few require additional documentation (medical fitness certificate from a registered medical practitioner) for drivers over 65, particularly for long outstation trips. Comprehensive insurance premiums may also be higher for senior drivers — but this is built into the rental price, not charged separately.</p>
