    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Route options and key milestones</h2><p>NH66 coastal route (about 560 km, 10-11 hours) is the standard Mumbai-Goa road and has good tarmac most of the year. Milestones: Panvel, Mahad, Chiplun, Ratnagiri turn-off (about 270 km — a good lunch stop), Kankavli, Sawantwadi, Patradevi Goa border. The inland alternative via Pune and Kolhapur on NH48, then over the Amboli ghat, avoids the single-lane Konkan section but adds ghat driving of its own. Drive recommendation: start before 6 AM from Mumbai to clear the city before morning peak; reach Ratnagiri by early afternoon for a break; Goa by evening. Monsoon (July-August) warning: NH66 through Konkan has landslide risk near Ratnagiri and Khed — check MoRTH or Google Maps alerts before the trip.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Mumbai to Goa trip cost breakdown</h2><p>Car booking: an SUV (Creta, Nexon, Brezza) for 3 days runs ₹7,500-12,000 (₹2,500-4,000 per day). Fuel: the round trip is about 1,120 km; at 11 km/L and ₹103/L, that is about ₹10,488. Tolls: tolls vary by route and vehicle class and are paid via FASTag — check current rates in your FASTag or NHAI app before you leave. Security deposit (refundable): ₹8,000-12,000, paid with the booking and refunded by Zymo after the trip. Total non-refundable trip cost for 2 people: approximately ₹17,988-22,488 plus tolls, against ₹31,360 for an intercity cab there and back (₹15,680 each way at ₹14/km, charged both ways).</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Best season and what to carry</h2><p>Best time to drive Mumbai to Goa: October to February. Roads are clear, Goa weather is mild, beaches are full. March to May is passable but hot — the ghats and Konkan strip get to 38-40°C. June to September monsoon: NH66 has landslide debris risk near Ratnagiri and on the Kashedi ghat — avoid unless you have an SUV and check real-time alerts. What to carry: driving licence originals, partner rental agreement (partner hands this over at pickup), Zymo booking confirmation on phone, FASTag works on the car (fitted by partner), first-aid kit. Google Maps works all the way to Patradevi Goa border; Airtel and Jio coverage holds on NH66 with minor dead spots in the ghat sections.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Panvel (about 50 km): last Mumbai suburb fuel stop — fill up fully here before heading south on NH66. Mahad and Chiplun: fuel stations on the highway, good pit stops. Ratnagiri turn-off (about 270 km): midpoint, recommended lunch stop. Sawantwadi (540 km): last major fuel stop before the Goa border — fill up here. Road conditions: NH66 from Panvel to Goa is 4-lane divided highway for most of the route; 2-lane with passing bays near Ratnagiri and Khed — stay in lane, no overtaking on blind curves.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for this route on Zymo</h2><p>Compact SUV (Hyundai Creta, Tata Nexon, Maruti Brezza): best for Mumbai-Goa in all seasons — ground clearance handles the Konkan coastal undulations and monsoon road debris. Higher seating gives better visibility on the mountain stretches. Toyota Innova Crysta: best for families of 5-6 with luggage — boot space for 4 large bags and comfortable rear seat. Hatchback (Swift, i20): acceptable in October-May dry weather on NH66; avoid in monsoon as the ghat section requires more torque and clearance than entry-level hatchbacks offer. Book on Zymo with Andheri, Powai, or Thane partners for the best Mumbai-departure inventory.</p></section>
