    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Mumbai to Lonavala is 83 km via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, approximately 1.5 hours. Lonavala-Khandala is Maharashtra's most popular quick getaway — Bhushi Dam, Tiger's Leap viewpoint, Rajmachi Fort, Karla and Bhaja Buddhist caves. A hatchback handles the route comfortably. Trip budget: car ₹1,100 + fuel ₹580 + tolls ₹300.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Mumbai to Lonavala route and what to see</h2><p>Mumbai → Khopoli toll (NH48 Expressway, 60 km, 45 mins): take the Khopoli exit to enter the old Pune road and access Lonavala via the ghat. Lonavala (83 km, 1.5 hrs from Andheri): Bhushi Dam (1 km from town, best July-October when water flows), Tiger's Leap (2.5 km from Lonavala — panoramic valley view), Sunset Point and Lion's Point (4-8 km). Khandala (3 km from Lonavala): Duke's Nose, Shooting Point. Karla Caves (11 km from Lonavala): 2,000-year-old Buddhist rock-cut caves. Rajmachi Fort (15 km): trekking destination — the road to Rajmachi Gate is unpaved.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Mumbai-Lonavala self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,100-1,500/day. Day trip: ₹1,100-1,500. Fuel: approximately 166 km round trip; hatchback at 15 km/L = ₹1,160. Tolls: Expressway (Khopoli toll ₹155 one-way) + ghat approach (₹40) = ₹195 one-way, ₹390 round-trip. Total day-trip budget for 2 people: ₹3,500-5,000. If doing an overnight (peak season): accommodation ₹2,000-6,000/night in Lonavala hill resorts.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Season guide and driving tips for Lonavala</h2><p>Monsoon July-October: Lonavala's most spectacular season — Bhushi Dam overflows, waterfalls appear, valleys are green. This is also the busiest and most congested period — depart Mumbai before 6 AM on weekends. November-February: pleasant, less crowded, good viewpoint visibility. March-June: hot but popular for resort stays (pools). Monsoon driving note: the old Pune road (NH4 via Khandala ghat) has beautiful waterfall views but is 2-lane with traffic — the Expressway is faster. Lonavala town traffic: very congested on weekend afternoons; park at the Lonavala Municipal parking lot near Bhushi Dam road.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Thane (20 km from Mumbai): fill up before the Expressway — fewer fuel options on the 6-lane. Khopoli (60 km): Expressway rest area has a fuel station — alternative refuelling point. Lonavala town: fuel station near the railway station. Road conditions: Mumbai-Lonavala via Expressway is 6-lane excellent; Khopoli-Lonavala old Pune road (NH4) is 2-lane but good; Lonavala interior roads to viewpoints are narrow 1.5-lane.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Mumbai-Lonavala on Zymo</h2><p>Maruti Swift or i20: perfect for Lonavala — the Expressway is flat and the viewpoint roads are narrow, where a compact car is easier than a larger vehicle. Hatchback fits Bhushi Dam parking (very narrow approach lane in monsoon). Compact SUV: warranted only if doing the Rajmachi Fort trek approach or the Pawna Lake camping road (unpaved). Book from Andheri, Borivali, or Thane Zymo partners for the closest Mumbai departure.</p></section>
