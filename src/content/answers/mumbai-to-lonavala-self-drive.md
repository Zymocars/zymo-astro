    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Mumbai to Lonavala is about 85 km via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, approximately 2 hours. Lonavala-Khandala is a popular quick getaway — Bhushi Dam, Tiger's Leap viewpoint, Rajmachi Fort, Karla and Bhaja Buddhist caves. A hatchback handles the route comfortably. Trip budget: car from ₹1,200 + fuel about ₹1,094 for the round trip, plus tolls.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Mumbai to Lonavala route and what to see</h2><p>Mumbai → Khalapur toll (Mumbai-Pune Expressway, about 60 km, 45 mins): take the Khopoli exit to enter the old Pune road and access Lonavala via the ghat. Lonavala (about 85 km, 2 hrs from Andheri): Bhushi Dam (1 km from town, best July-October when water flows), Tiger's Leap (2.5 km from Lonavala — panoramic valley view), Sunset Point and Lion's Point (4-8 km). Khandala (3 km from Lonavala): Duke's Nose, Shooting Point. Karla Caves (11 km from Lonavala): 2,000-year-old Buddhist rock-cut caves. Rajmachi Fort (15 km): trekking destination — the road to Rajmachi Gate is unpaved.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Mumbai-Lonavala self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,200-1,800/day. Day trip: ₹1,200-1,800. Fuel: approximately 170 km round trip; a hatchback at 16 km/L and ₹103/L = about ₹1,094. Tolls: tolls vary by route and vehicle class and are paid via FASTag — check current rates in your FASTag or NHAI app before you leave. Car and fuel for a day trip: about ₹2,294-2,894, plus tolls and food. If doing an overnight (peak season): accommodation ₹2,000-6,000/night in Lonavala hill resorts.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Season guide and driving tips for Lonavala</h2><p>Monsoon July-October: Lonavala's most spectacular season — Bhushi Dam overflows, waterfalls appear, valleys are green. This is also the busiest and most congested period — depart Mumbai before 6 AM on weekends. November-February: pleasant, less crowded, good viewpoint visibility. March-June: hot but popular for resort stays (pools). Monsoon driving note: the old Pune road (NH4 via Khandala ghat) has beautiful waterfall views but is 2-lane with traffic — the Expressway is faster. Lonavala town traffic: very congested on weekend afternoons; use paid parking in town.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Panvel/Kalamboli, at the Expressway start: fill up here — fewer fuel options on the 6-lane. Khalapur food plaza (about 60 km): fuel station — alternative refuelling point. Lonavala town: fuel station near the railway station. Road conditions: Mumbai-Lonavala via Expressway is 6-lane excellent; Khopoli-Lonavala old Pune road (NH4) is 2-lane but good; Lonavala interior roads to viewpoints are narrow 1.5-lane.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Mumbai-Lonavala on Zymo</h2><p>Maruti Swift or i20: perfect for Lonavala — the Expressway is flat and the viewpoint roads are narrow, where a compact car is easier than a larger vehicle. Hatchback fits Bhushi Dam parking (very narrow approach lane in monsoon). Compact SUV: warranted only if doing the Rajmachi Fort trek approach or the Pawna Lake camping road (unpaved). Book from Andheri, Borivali, or Thane Zymo partners for the closest Mumbai departure.</p></section>
