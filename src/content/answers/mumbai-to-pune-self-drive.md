    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Mumbai to Pune is 150 km via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, approximately 2-2.5 hours. One of India's most-driven intercity routes — 6-lane divided expressway with a midpoint food plaza near Khalapur. Mumbai-Pune Expressway toll: ₹320 one way for a car (as of October 2026), paid via FASTag. A hatchback or sedan handles this route perfectly — no SUV needed.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Mumbai to Pune route breakdown</h2><p>Mumbai (various zones) → Vashi → Panvel/Kalamboli, where the Expressway begins → Khalapur toll (about 60 km, 1 hr): exit at Khopoli if taking the Lonavala detour. Lonavala (optional 30 km detour): Tiger's Leap viewpoint, Khandala, Bhushi Dam — add 1.5 hours round trip. Pune (150 km total, 2-2.5 hrs). Alternative Old NH4 via Khopoli-Khandala-Lonavala: scenic but 45 minutes slower, passes through ghat town bazaars. Tip: depart Mumbai before 7 AM on weekdays or before 6 AM on Fridays to avoid the Vashi-Panvel approach jam.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Mumbai-Pune self-drive</h2><p>Car: hatchback ₹1,200-1,800/day. Day trip: ₹1,200-1,800. 2-day Pune weekend: car ₹2,400-3,600. Fuel: 300 km round trip; hatchback at 15 km/L at ₹105/L = ₹2,100. Tolls: ₹320 one way for a car (as of October 2026), ₹640 round trip, paid via FASTag. Car, fuel, and Expressway tolls for 2 days: about ₹5,100-6,300. The Expressway reduces trip cost significantly over the old NH4 which adds 30 minutes and fuel.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to do on a Mumbai-Pune self-drive</h2><p>Lonavala-Khandala (about 85 km from Mumbai, just off the Expressway): Bhushi Dam (best in monsoon), Tiger's Leap viewpoint, Karla and Bhaja Buddhist caves nearby. Pune Shaniwar Wada: Peshwa palace — half-day sightseeing. Aga Khan Palace: Mahatma Gandhi memorial, 6 km from city centre. Sinhagad Fort: 30 km southwest of Pune — 1 km steep uphill walk, panoramic Sahyadri views. Lavasa hill city: 60 km from Pune on the Pune-Tamhini Ghat road — European-style lakeside development.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Kalamboli, at the Expressway start: last city fuel before the 6-lane expressway — fill up here if starting on low. Khalapur food plaza (about 60 km): fuel station and restaurants — standard midpoint break for Mumbai-Pune commuters. Urse toll (near Talegaon): fuel stations near the expressway exit. Road conditions: the Mumbai-Pune Expressway is 6-lane with an excellent surface, speed cameras at Khopoli and Khandala (drive at 100 km/h limit). Monsoon warning: Khandala-Lonavala old road (if taking the scenic route) has landslide debris risk July-August — use Expressway in monsoon.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Mumbai-Pune on Zymo</h2><p>Maruti Swift or Hyundai i20: the default choice for Mumbai-Pune — fuel-efficient on the flat Expressway, easy to park in Pune's market areas. Sedan (Dzire, Honda Amaze): slightly more rear comfort if 4 adults. You genuinely do not need an SUV for this route — it is a flat 6-lane highway. Only upgrade if extending the trip to Mahabaleshwar (mountain roads) or the Sahyadri forts. Book from Andheri, Thane, or Borivali partners on Zymo for the most convenient Mumbai-departure pickup.</p></section>
