    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">One-day self-drive trip ideas from India's major cities</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">A one-day self-drive from any major Indian city covers 60-200 km each way — enough for a heritage site, hill station, beach, or wildlife sanctuary. Hatchback day-trip cost: ₹1,100 (car) + ₹700-2,100 (fuel depending on distance) + tolls. Depart before 6 AM to maximise time at the destination. Here are the best one-day self-drive ideas from six cities.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Best one-day drives from Mumbai</h2><p>Lonavala (83 km, 1.5 hrs): Bhushi Dam, Tiger's Leap, Karla Caves — depart 5:30 AM, return by 9 PM. Alibaug (45 km drive + Mandwa ferry, 2 hrs total): beach town, Kulaba Fort, Alibag beach. Nashik (165 km, 3 hrs): Sula Vineyards + Trimbakeshwar in one day — depart 5 AM, return by 9 PM. Mahabaleshwar is possible as a day trip (280 km, 5.5 hrs) but accommodation overnight is far more relaxing. Car: hatchback for Lonavala/Nashik; SUV for Mahabaleshwar ghat.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Best one-day drives from Delhi</h2><p>Agra (250 km, 3.5 hrs Yamuna Expressway): Taj Mahal + Agra Fort — depart 5 AM, reach Agra by 8:30 AM for sunrise Taj, return by 8 PM. Mathura-Vrindavan (170 km, 2.5 hrs): Krishna pilgrimage circuit, easier than Agra. Rishikesh (240 km, 5 hrs): white-water rafting — depart 4 AM. Haridwar (215 km, 4 hrs): Har Ki Pauri Ganga Aarti — feasible day trip. Car: sedan for Agra/Mathura; hatchback for Haridwar/Rishikesh.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Best one-day drives from Bangalore</h2><p>Mysore (150 km, 2.5 hrs NH275): Mysore Palace + Chamundi Hill + Brindavan Gardens. Best day trip from Bangalore — depart 6 AM, return by 9 PM comfortably. Coorg Madikeri (250 km, 5 hrs): tight as a day trip but doable — depart 4:30 AM. Mahabalipuram from Chennai detour (not Bangalore): see Chennai section. Car: hatchback for Mysore. Coorg needs either an early 4:30 AM start or an overnight.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Best one-day drives from Chennai, Hyderabad, and Pune</h2><p>From Chennai: Mahabalipuram (55 km, 1 hr ECR): UNESCO Shore Temple — the easiest day trip in South India. Pondicherry extension (155 km, 3 hrs): full day trip, French Quarter + beaches. From Hyderabad: Warangal (145 km, 2.5 hrs): Ramappa Temple UNESCO + Warangal Fort — comfortable day trip. Nagarjunasagar (165 km, 3 hrs): dam and scenic gorge. From Pune: Lonavala (65 km, 1 hr): Bhushi Dam and viewpoints. Mahabaleshwar (120 km, 2.5 hrs): hill station — more comfortable as a 2-day.</p></section>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How many km can I cover in a one-day self-drive trip?</dt>
    <dd>Realistically 300-500 km round trip in 8-10 hours of driving. Factor in 2-4 hours at the destination — so ideal destinations are 100-200 km from your city. For 300 km+ day trips, leave by 5-6 AM.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the cost of a one-day self-drive trip?</dt>
    <dd>Typically ₹2,500-5,000: car ₹1,200-2,500 (1 day rental) + fuel ₹700-1,500 (200-350 km round trip at ₹100/litre petrol) + tolls ₹200-500 + destination entry/food. Much cheaper than guided tours for the same destinations.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What car should I book for a one-day self-drive trip?</dt>
    <dd>Hatchback (Swift, i20, WagonR) for solo or couples — lowest cost and easy parking at hill stations and heritage sites. Compact SUV (Creta, Nexon) for families or mixed terrain. Avoid booking an MPV or large SUV for a solo day trip — overkill.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I book a half-day (4-hour) car for a quick day trip?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Zymo partners offer 4-hour and 6-hour half-day bookings in most cities. Destinations within 50 km are perfectly doable on a half-day rental: Nandi Hills from Bangalore (60 km), Mahabalipuram from Chennai (55 km), Lonavala from Pune (65 km).</dd>
