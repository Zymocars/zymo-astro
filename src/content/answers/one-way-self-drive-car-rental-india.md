    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Can I book a one-way self-drive car rental in India?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">One-way self-drive bookings are supported by some Zymo partners on popular metro-to-metro routes (Mumbai-Goa, Delhi-Jaipur, Bangalore-Chennai). Availability is partner-by-partner — not every partner offers one-way drops. The drop fee for a one-way booking is higher than a round trip because the partner has to relocate the car back. Search your route on Zymo to see live one-way availability.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How one-way booking works</h2><p>Pick your pickup city, choose the drop city on the booking page (where supported by the partner), and the system shows partners that offer the drop along with the one-way fee. The fee covers the partner's cost of relocating the car back. Documents and security deposit work the same way as a round-trip booking. The car is dropped at the destination city's partner pickup point.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Routes where one-way works best</h2><p>High-volume metro pairs — Mumbai-Goa, Delhi-Jaipur, Delhi-Agra, Bangalore-Mysore, Bangalore-Chennai, Mumbai-Pune — have the most one-way options because partners have inventory at both ends. Less common destination pairs (small Tier-2 to small Tier-3) often only offer round-trip, since the partner cannot easily place the car back.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost comparison vs round trip</h2><p>One-way bookings cost more than the equivalent round-trip — typically 1.3 to 1.8× the daily rate plus a fixed drop fee. For trips longer than 5 days or for groups planning to fly back, the one-way premium can still be cheaper than the round-trip total. Confirm both options on the booking page for your specific dates.</p></section>
    
    
    
    <section id="faq">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Related questions</h2>
    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Do all Zymo partners offer one-way bookings?</dt>
    <dd>No. One-way availability is partner-by-partner. Search your pickup city and select the drop city on the booking page; the system shows only partners that support that specific one-way pair.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Why is a one-way booking more expensive than round-trip?</dt>
    <dd>The partner has to relocate the car back to its base after you drop it. The drop fee covers that relocation cost. For high-volume metro pairs (Mumbai-Goa, Delhi-Jaipur, Bangalore-Mysore), the drop fee is lower because the partner has inventory at both ends.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I drop the car at a different city's airport on a one-way?</dt>
    <dd>Some partners support airport drop on one-way bookings. Confirm on the booking page — airport drop typically carries a small additional fee over the standard one-way drop point.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What documents do I need for a one-way booking?</dt>
    <dd>Same as a round-trip booking: valid Indian driving licence, government photo ID, payment card for security deposit. Inter-state travel doesn't require additional driver permits but may incur a one-time inter-state tax at the border.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I extend a one-way booking once underway?</dt>
    <dd>Yes, subject to partner availability. Use the in-app extension flow; the additional time is billed at the partner's daily rate plus a pro-rata drop-fee adjustment if the new drop time changes the relocation schedule.</dd>
    </dl>
