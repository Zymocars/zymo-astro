    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Pune to Goa self-drive road trip — complete guide</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Pune to Goa is about 430 km via NH48 to Kolhapur, then over the Amboli ghat to Sawantwadi and Goa, approximately 7-8 hours. The route passes through Satara, Kolhapur, and crosses into Goa at Patradevi. An SUV is recommended for the Sahyadri approach roads near Kolhapur; a hatchback handles the NH all the way. Budget: car ₹4,400-6,000 for 2 days, fuel about ₹8,200, plus tolls.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Pune to Goa route breakdown</h2><p>Pune → Satara via NH48 (115 km, 2 hrs): 4-lane, good surface. Satara → Kolhapur via NH48 (115 km, 2 hrs): good 4-lane road through sugarcane and agri country. Kolhapur → Sawantwadi via the Amboli ghat (135 km, 2.5 hrs): the road narrows near Amboli. Sawantwadi → Panaji via the Patradevi border (about 65 km, 1.5 hrs). Total: about 430 km, 7-8 hours. Depart Pune before 6 AM for a mid-afternoon Goa arrival.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Pune-Goa self-drive</h2><p>Car: compact SUV ₹2,200-3,000/day × 2 days = ₹4,400-6,000. Fuel: about 860 km round trip; petrol SUV at 11 km/L at ₹105/L = about ₹8,200. Tolls: tolls vary by route and vehicle class and are paid via FASTag — check current rates in your FASTag or NHAI app before you leave. Car and fuel: about ₹12,600-14,200, plus tolls and your stay in Goa.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Season and route conditions</h2><p>November to March: best for Goa — beaches are open, weather pleasant. The route is clear in this window. April-May: hot through the Deccan interior but Goa beaches manageable until mid-April. June-September monsoon: the Kolhapur-Sawantwadi road has landslide risk near Amboli ghat — check Maharashtra PWD alerts. Kolhapur itself receives heavy monsoon rain. Best departure window: depart Pune at 5-6 AM to clear Kolhapur before noon heat.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Satara (115 km from Pune): mid-point fuel and breakfast — multiple stations on NH48. Kolhapur (230 km): essential fuel stop before the Goa approach road — fill full tank here. Sawantwadi (365 km): small town before Goa border, fuel available. Road conditions: Pune-Kolhapur NH48 is 4-lane good; Kolhapur-Sawantwadi is a narrower ghat road through Amboli — slower driving expected. Goa interior roads: compact and fine for all cars.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Pune-Goa on Zymo</h2><p>Compact SUV (Creta, Brezza, Nexon): recommended — comfortable for 2 adults on the 7-hour drive and capable on the Amboli ghat approach if exploring Sawantwadi area. Hatchback (Swift, Baleno): manages the route in October-May dry season; avoid during monsoon on the Amboli section. Maruti Ertiga: 7-seater ideal for a group of 5-6 making Pune-Goa, reasonable boot space. Book from Hinjewadi or Koregaon Park Zymo partners for Pune-departure pickup.</p></section>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Which route is better — NH48 via Kolhapur or Konkan Highway?</dt>
    <dd>NH48 via Kolhapur is faster (7-8 hours, mostly 4-lane). NH166 via Satara-Ratnagiri is slower (9-10 hours) but spectacular — coastal Konkan views and better if you want to stop at beaches like Ganpatipule.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the toll from Pune to Goa?</dt>
    <dd>Tolls on NH48 and the Goa approach vary by vehicle class and are revised periodically — check the current total in your FASTag or NHAI app before you leave.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What car is best for Pune to Goa?</dt>
    <dd>Compact SUV (Creta, Nexon, Seltos) for 4-5 people — good highway cruising, boot for 5 days of luggage, and handles both NH and Goa beach roads well. Innova Crysta for families of 6.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does Pune to Goa self-drive cost?</dt>
    <dd>Approximately ₹19,000-29,000 for 3 days: car ₹6,600-9,000 (compact SUV, 3 days) + fuel about ₹8,200 (860 km at 11 km/L and ₹105/L) + tolls + Goa accommodation ₹4,000-12,000.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I book a Pune self-drive car for Goa?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Confirm Goa outstation permission with the partner at booking.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What should I see on the Pune-Goa route via NH48?</dt>
    <dd>Kolhapur Mahalakshmi Temple (230 km from Pune, en-route) is worth a 2-hour stop. Amboli Ghat (about 30 km from Sawantwadi) is a stunning viewpoint with waterfalls in monsoon and post-monsoon seasons.</dd>
