    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Pune to Goa is 455 km via NH48 to Kolhapur then NH48/NH66 to Goa, approximately 7-8 hours. The route passes through Satara, Kolhapur, and crosses into Goa at Patradevi. An SUV is recommended for the Sahyadri approach roads near Kolhapur; a hatchback handles the NH all the way. Budget: car ₹4,400-6,000 for 2 days, fuel ₹9,500, tolls ₹900.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Pune to Goa route breakdown</h2><p>Pune → Satara via NH48 (115 km, 2 hrs): 4-lane, good surface. Satara → Kolhapur via NH48 (115 km, 2 hrs): good 4-lane road through sugarcane and agri country. Kolhapur → Sawantwadi via NH66 (90 km, 1.5 hrs): the Konkan ghat section starts — road narrows near Amboli ghat. Sawantwadi → Patradevi Goa border (50 km, 45 mins). Total: 455 km, 7-8 hours. Depart Pune before 6 AM for a mid-afternoon Goa arrival.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost breakdown for Pune-Goa self-drive</h2><p>Car: compact SUV ₹2,200-3,000/day × 2 days = ₹4,400-6,000. Fuel: 910 km round trip; petrol SUV at 11 km/L at ₹105/L = ₹8,700 — budget ₹9,000. Tolls: NH48 Pune-Kolhapur (₹280 one-way) + NH66 Kolhapur-Goa (₹170) — one-way total ₹450, round-trip ₹900. Total non-refundable trip cost for 2 people: approximately ₹18,000-24,000 for 2 nights in Goa.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Season and route conditions</h2><p>November to March: best for Goa — beaches are open, weather pleasant. NH48 and NH66 are clear in this window. April-May: hot through the Deccan interior but Goa beaches manageable until mid-April. June-September monsoon: NH66 through the Konkan between Kolhapur and Sawantwadi has landslide risk near Amboli ghat — check Maharashtra PWD alerts. Kolhapur itself receives heavy monsoon rain. Best departure window: depart Pune at 5-6 AM to clear Kolhapur before noon heat.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fuel stops and road conditions by segment</h2><p>Satara (115 km from Pune): mid-point fuel and breakfast — multiple stations on NH48. Kolhapur (230 km): essential fuel stop before the Goa approach road — fill full tank here. Sawantwadi (365 km): small town before Goa border, fuel available. Road conditions: Pune-Kolhapur NH48 is 4-lane good; Kolhapur-Goa NH66 is 2-4 lane with ghat narrowing near Amboli — slower driving expected. Goa interior roads: compact and fine for all cars.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which car to book for Pune-Goa on Zymo</h2><p>Compact SUV (Creta, Brezza, Nexon): recommended — comfortable for 2 adults on the 7-hour drive and capable on the Amboli ghat approach if exploring Sawantwadi area. Hatchback (Swift, Baleno): manages the route in October-May dry season; avoid during monsoon on the Amboli section. Maruti Ertiga: 7-seater ideal for a group of 5-6 making Pune-Goa, reasonable boot space. Book from Hinjewadi or Koregaon Park Zymo partners for Pune-departure pickup.</p></section>
