Through an Extended Test Drive on Zymo, you can book the exact model you are considering buying — Hyundai Creta, Tata Nexon, Maruti Brezza, Kia Seltos, and more — for anywhere from one day to a month and drive it on your own routes, parking slot, and weekend before you sign at the showroom. The car is handed over at a partner location near you. You drive it as you normally would — your daily commute, your weekend errand, your highway stretch, the parking slot at home. No salesperson next to you. No manufactured test circuit. After the booking, you return the car and head to the showroom already knowing whether you want to buy.</p></section>

Whether the infotainment lag bothers you by day two. Whether the second row has enough legroom for your parents or children on a 3-hour trip. Whether the turning radius fits your society's basement parking. What the AC load sounds like in a June traffic jam. What the car feels like at 5 AM on a tired morning. None of these reveal themselves on a smooth road near the showroom.</p></section>
