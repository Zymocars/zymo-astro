    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">What to do if your self-drive rental car isn't at the pickup point in India?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">If your self-drive booking isn't at the pickup point at the scheduled time, contact Zymo customer care at hello@zymo.app with your booking ID. If the partner cannot deliver the booked car, Zymo initiates a full refund of the trip cost — bank settlement takes 5–7 working days. Zymo does not automatically provide a replacement vehicle, but customer care can help you search for an alternative on Zymo on request. For urgent travel needs, book a fresh alternative on Zymo or another platform now and rely on the refund to offset the original booking's cost.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">First 30 minutes at the pickup point — what to verify</h2><p>Confirm the booking details on the Zymo app before deciding the partner has no-showed. Common false-alarm causes: (1) pickup location on the booking page is different from where you're waiting — some partners use a hub address (e.g. 'Andheri MIDC gate') that isn't the exact street corner you're standing on; (2) partner is running 20-30 minutes late but reachable — call the partner-contact number on your booking receipt; (3) car was allotted a different colour/plate than expected but is present; (4) partner requires a document check at their office before releasing the car, which takes 30-45 minutes. If after 30 minutes the partner is unreachable, not present, and hasn't sent any communication, treat it as a no-show and escalate to Zymo.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The Zymo escalation path</h2><p>Email hello@zymo.app with your booking ID as the subject line and the specifics: pickup date and time, pickup location on your booking page, what you found when you arrived, and whether the partner is reachable. If the partner cannot deliver the car, Zymo initiates a full refund of the trip cost — Cashfree settlement takes 5–7 working days for the refund to appear in your original payment method. If your travel window is time-sensitive, mention it in the escalation email — customer care can help you search for an alternative partner car in the same city on priority. Note that Zymo does not automatically provide a replacement vehicle; the alternative search is a manual courtesy on request.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to protect against no-show risk before it happens</h2><p>Book at least 24 hours before pickup rather than same-hour where possible — partners have more time to confirm inventory. Screenshot the Zymo booking page after payment showing the partner name, pickup address, contact number, and confirmed time — this is your evidence in a dispute. Call the partner's contact number the evening before pickup to confirm they have your booking on their board — a 60-second call surfaces most no-show risks before pickup day. Doorstep delivery bookings have higher no-show risk than hub-pickup bookings because they add a delivery-time variable; if reliability matters more than convenience, hub-pickup is safer. Finally: don't buy non-refundable travel (flights, hotels) that assume the self-drive car will pick up on time — treat the car as one variable in the itinerary, and have a Plan B for the leg you were going to drive.</p></section>

    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What happens if my Zymo self-drive rental isn't at the pickup point?</dt>
    <dd>Contact Zymo customer care at hello@zymo.app with your booking ID. If the partner cannot deliver the booked car, Zymo initiates a full refund of the trip cost — 5–7 working days for bank settlement.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Does Zymo automatically provide a replacement car if the partner no-shows?</dt>
    <dd>No. Zymo does not automatically provide a replacement vehicle. The primary remedy is a full refund. If you mention time-sensitive travel in your escalation email to hello@zymo.app, customer care can help you search for an alternative partner car on priority — but this is a manual courtesy on request, not an automatic guarantee. For urgent travel, book a fresh alternative on Zymo or another platform immediately and rely on the refund to offset the original booking cost.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How do I escalate if my rental doesn't show up?</dt>
    <dd>Email hello@zymo.app with your booking ID as soon as you find the car missing. If your pickup is the same day, mark 'URGENT' in the subject line — customer care prioritises time-sensitive escalations. For extreme cases (e.g. partner attempting extortion, safety concern), the founder handles the escalation personally.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How long before I should treat a partner delay as a no-show?</dt>
    <dd>30 minutes past the scheduled pickup time with no communication from the partner is the typical no-show threshold. Before escalating, confirm three things: (1) you're at the exact pickup location shown on your booking page (partners often use hub addresses that differ from a specific street corner); (2) you've called the partner-contact number on the booking receipt; (3) the partner hasn't sent a delay message via WhatsApp or SMS. If all three check out and the partner is still absent/unreachable at 30 minutes past, escalate to Zymo.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Will Zymo compensate me beyond the refund if the partner no-shows?</dt>
    <dd>The standard remedy is a full refund of the trip cost. If the no-show caused significant downstream costs (e.g. missed a flight, had to book an expensive last-minute alternative), share the specifics in your escalation email — Zymo customer care reviews case-by-case. Any goodwill offer beyond the refund requires founder approval and is decided on individual merit; there is no automated compensation matrix.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I prevent no-show risk before pickup day?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Three practices reduce the risk substantially: (1) call the partner-contact number the evening before pickup to confirm the booking is on their board — a 60-second call surfaces most issues before pickup day; (2) read the partner's policies on the booking page before you confirm; (3) prefer hub-pickup over doorstep-delivery when reliability matters more than convenience, since delivery adds a time variable. Also book 24+ hours before pickup rather than same-hour where possible — partners have more inventory-confirmation buffer.</dd>
