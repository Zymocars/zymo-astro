    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">How long does it take to get a self-drive car deposit refund in India?</h1>
    <p style="color:#8A94A6;font-size:0.85rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem">Updated June 2026 &middot; Based on partner policies across the Zymo network</p>
    
    
    
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Zymo refunds the security deposit on self-drive bookings within 5–7 working days after the trip, once the car is returned without damage and with the correct fuel level. The deposit is paid online with your booking and held by Zymo during the rental period; Zymo refunds it to the same payment method after the return is checked.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How the deposit actually works</h2><p>The security deposit (typically ₹3,000 to ₹15,000 depending on car class) is paid online with your booking through Zymo's payment gateway, alongside the rental amount. Zymo holds it during the trip — the partner does not collect a deposit at pickup, in cash or otherwise. After you return the car, the partner reports its condition and fuel level, Zymo applies any documented deductions, and refunds the balance to your original payment method. The deposit amount is shown on the booking page before you pay.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">When a deposit refund gets delayed</h2><p>Zymo refunds the deposit within 5–7 working days after the trip for a clean return. A deduction for fuel, damage, a late return, or tolls and traffic fines is never taken silently: it is raised as a post-trip charge that Zymo sends you to review. Delays beyond 5–7 working days trace to one of four causes: (1) a post-trip charge is waiting for your review — you have 48 hours to approve or dispute it, and a charge you do not answer is settled from the deposit; (2) you disputed a post-trip charge — Zymo resolves a disputed charge in 3–5 working days, then refunds the balance; (3) the partner's return check of fuel level and condition is not yet complete; (4) a settlement lag at your bank or card issuer. If your refund is not credited within 7 working days after the trip, message Zymo through the Zai chatbot or at hello@zymo.app with your booking ID. Keep the partner's post-return inspection photograph or acknowledgement message as proof of clean return.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to protect your deposit before, during, and after</h2><p>Before pickup: photograph the car from all angles (front, back, both sides, roof, interior dashboard, odometer, fuel gauge) and confirm any pre-existing damage on the partner's handover form. This is your baseline. During the trip: keep FASTag balance topped up to avoid post-trip toll deductions; drive to the partner's stated km limit or budget for extra-km charges upfront; refuel to the exact level specified in the fuel policy on the booking page. After return: get the partner to acknowledge the fuel level, odometer reading, and vehicle condition in writing (WhatsApp, email, or a signed handover form). Screenshot the acknowledgement. Any deduction is raised as a post-trip charge: you have 48 hours to approve or dispute it, a charge you do not answer is settled from the deposit, and a disputed charge is resolved in 3–5 working days. This evidence is what you send when you dispute.</p></section>
