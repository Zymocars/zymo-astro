    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the minimum age for self-drive car rental in Delhi?</dt>
    <dd>Minimum age depends on the partner — usually 21 for hatchbacks and sedans, and some accept 18. Compact SUVs (Creta, Seltos, Nexon) typically require 21-23 years; full-size SUVs (Fortuner, XUV700) may require 23-25. Each car shows its requirement before you book.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I take a self-drive car from Delhi to Manali?</dt>
    <dd>Yes, with the right car. Delhi to Manali is 535 km via NH44 and NH3. An SUV is mandatory for monsoon and October-February travel. Confirm at booking that the partner's outstation permit covers Himachal Pradesh. Book at zymo.app and declare Manali as the destination during partner handover.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does a self-drive car cost for a weekend in Delhi?</dt>
    <dd>A weekend booking (2 days) in Delhi: hatchback ₹2,200-3,000 total, compact SUV ₹4,400-6,000 total. Weekend pricing can run 10-15% higher than weekday rates on peak demand days. Fuel, tolls, and parking are additional. Security deposit (refundable) is also held at pickup.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Are self-drive pickups available in Gurgaon and Noida?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Zymo partners in Gurgaon cover DLF Phase 1-3, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, and the Cyber Hub area. Noida partners cover Sectors 18, 62, and 137. Search your specific sector or neighbourhood on Zymo to see live partner availability.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is it cheaper to rent a self-drive car than booking Ola or Uber in Delhi?</dt>
    <dd>For trips over 150 km with 2+ passengers, self-drive is meaningfully cheaper. Delhi-Agra in an Ola Outstation cab runs ₹3,500-4,500 one-way; the same trip in a self-drive hatchback runs ₹1,100 booking + ₹1,800 fuel + ₹405 tolls = ₹3,305 round-trip cost split across 2 passengers. Solo short-city trips are often cheaper in Ola/Uber.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I take a self-drive car from Delhi to Agra?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Delhi to Agra is 210 km on NH44 and takes 3-4 hours. Yamuna Expressway toll is ₹300 one-way. A 2-day booking covers Delhi-Agra-Jaipur comfortably — one of the most popular outstation routes from Delhi.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What documents do I need for a self-drive rental in Delhi?</dt>
    <dd>A valid driving licence from any state — Delhi does not require a Delhi-issued licence for self-drive. Aadhaar OTP is required on Zymo for first-time customers. International driving licences are accepted on most platforms for foreign nationals staying legally in India.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Are there self-drive electric cars available in Delhi?</dt>
