    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is it worth renting a self-drive car in Goa?</dt>
    <dd>Yes — Goa has no metro or reliable public transport between beaches. A self-drive car lets you move between North and South Goa at your own pace and is cheaper than hiring a taxi for 3+ days.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does a self-drive car cost in Goa per day?</dt>
    <dd>Hatchbacks about ₹1,200-1,800/day. SUVs from ₹2,600. Fuel and security deposit are separate. Local taxis in Goa typically charge ₹1,200-2,000 for a full day — comparable to a rental car if you drive a lot.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I pick up a self-drive car at Goa airport?</dt>
    <dd>Yes, where a partner offers it. Some partners offer pickup near both Dabolim (South Goa) and Mopa International Airport (North Goa). Confirm the pickup point on the booking page — some partners offer airport meet-and-greet.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Which car is best for exploring Goa?</dt>
    <dd>A hatchback is sufficient for standard Goa exploration — roads are narrow and parking is tight at beaches. If you plan to visit the interior (Dudhsagar, Mollem, Bondla), a compact SUV is more comfortable on the rough forest roads.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I drive from Goa to Mumbai in a rented self-drive car?</dt>
    <dd>Yes, if your Zymo booking allows outstation use and includes an inter-state permit for Maharashtra. Goa-Mumbai is about 560 km via NH66, approximately 10-11 hours. Confirm outstation permission at booking.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What documents do I need to rent a self-drive car in Goa?</dt>
    <dd>A valid driving licence (any state) and Aadhaar for Zymo KYC. Goa does not require a Goa-issued licence. Foreign nationals can use an international driving permit plus their home country licence.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Are self-drive cars available in North Goa (Calangute/Baga)?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Zymo has partners in Mapusa, Calangute, and the North Goa beach belt. Search your area for live availability.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is night driving safe in Goa?</dt>
    <dd>Night driving on the main coastal road (between Panaji and Calangute) is safe but watch for drink-drive checkpoints. Interior roads in Ponda and Molem can be unlit and have wildlife crossing points — avoid late-night drives on these routes.</dd>
