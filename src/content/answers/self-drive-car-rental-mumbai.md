    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What is the best area to pick up a self-drive car in Mumbai?</dt>
    <dd>Andheri (West or East) is a common pickup area with quick access to the Western Express Highway and the airport. For trips starting from the south or BKC, Bandra and Powai are convenient. Thane and Navi Mumbai partners are best if you are based in the MMR suburbs and heading east or south.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I drive a self-drive rental car from Mumbai to Goa?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Mumbai to Goa via NH66 is a standard outstation self-drive on Zymo. Confirm that your partner's booking includes outstation permit for Maharashtra-Goa border crossing. The trip is about 560 km, 10-11 hours; an SUV is recommended. Tolls are paid via FASTag.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How much does it cost to rent a self-drive car in Mumbai for a day?</dt>
    <dd>Hatchbacks (Swift, i20, WagonR) cost about ₹1,200-1,800 per day in Mumbai. Sedans (Dzire, Aura) from ₹1,800. SUVs (Brezza, Nexon, Creta) from ₹2,500. Hourly booking from ₹45 per hour for city-only use. Prices vary by date — weekends and holidays carry higher rates. Security deposit (refundable) is additional.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Which car class is best for Mumbai roads?</dt>
    <dd>For city-only Mumbai use, a hatchback or sedan is the most practical — easy to park in Bandra, BKC, and old city lanes. For the Mumbai-Goa or Mumbai-Nashik outstation run, a compact SUV with ground clearance (Brezza, Nexon, Creta) handles the Konkan and ghat stretches comfortably. Full-size SUVs (Fortuner, Scorpio) are overkill for Mumbai city use alone.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Are self-drive cars available in Navi Mumbai and Thane?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Zymo partners operate pickup points in Navi Mumbai (Vashi, Kharghar, Belapur) and Thane (Thane station area, Ghodbunder Road). Search your specific neighbourhood on Zymo to see live partner pickup locations and availability for your dates.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">What time should I pick up a self-drive car in Mumbai to avoid traffic?</dt>
    <dd>Pick up before 7 AM or after 8 PM. Rush-hour pickup (7-10 AM or 6-9 PM) in Andheri and Bandra adds 45-90 minutes of idle time. Some Zymo partners offer late-night doorstep delivery — confirm availability at booking if your timing is tight.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Do self-drive rental cars in Mumbai have FASTag installed?</dt>
    <dd>Yes, most Zymo partner cars in Mumbai come with FASTag pre-installed — covering the Mumbai-Pune Expressway (toll ₹320 one way for a car (as of October 2026)), Eastern Freeway, and Bandra-Worli Sea Link. Confirm with the partner at handover whether your FASTag account has sufficient balance.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I book a self-drive car in Mumbai for just one day for a day trip?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. One-day outstation bookings work well for Mumbai-Pune (about 150 km), Mumbai-Alibaug (about 95 km by road), or Mumbai-Nashik (165 km). For Mumbai-Goa (about 560 km one-way), book a minimum of 3 days.</dd>
