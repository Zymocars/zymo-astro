    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Self-drive rental scams in India follow a predictable set of red flags: cash-only demands at pickup or return, off-platform payment requests (UPI to a personal number, not the platform's payment gateway), threats of extra charges without written basis, refusal to sign or return a proper handover form, and pressure to pay 'safety deposits' outside the platform's booking flow. Booking through an aggregator like Zymo reduces exposure through three specific mechanisms: (1) every charge is disclosed on the booking page before you pay, (2) Aadhaar OTP verification is standard so the transaction has an identity trail, and (3) any partner accused of misconduct is hard-stop escalated to the founder for personal handling — not routed through a boilerplate customer-care flow.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Red flags to refuse at pickup</h2><p>If a partner asks for cash at pickup for anything not disclosed on your Zymo booking page — decline the payment. Every legitimate charge (rental, deposit, extra-km, delivery) is shown before payment. A pickup-time cash demand is either a scam or an off-platform surcharge attempt. If a partner asks you to pay via UPI to a personal Google Pay or PhonePe number — decline; Zymo's payment gateway is Cashfree, and legitimate booking amounts are settled at the time of booking, not at pickup. If a partner refuses to give you a written or app-based handover form documenting the car's condition, fuel level, and odometer reading — decline pickup; without this evidence, you have no defence against a later damage claim. If a partner attempts to add a 'security deposit' beyond the amount shown on your booking page — decline; the deposit is paid online with your booking and held by Zymo, not paid to the partner directly. If a partner threatens you with police involvement, RTO complaints, or 'consequences' unless you pay a demand — this is extortion behaviour, not a legitimate operational conversation.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How Zymo reduces scam exposure vs off-platform car rentals</h2><p>Three specific mechanisms make on-platform booking safer than off-platform (Facebook groups, WhatsApp-only partners, unverified local rental shops). First: every charge is disclosed before payment on the Zymo booking page — rental amount, security deposit, kilometre cap, extra-km rate, fuel policy, delivery fee where applicable. If a partner tries to add something at pickup that wasn't on the booking page, you have concrete grounds to refuse and escalate. Second: Aadhaar OTP verification is standard on every Zymo booking, which means both the customer and the transaction have an identity trail — this creates disincentive for either side to attempt fraud because impersonation and forged-document risks are much lower. Third: Zymo's dispute escalation path routes suspected partner misconduct hard-stop to the founder for personal handling. The customer-care team does not send boilerplate replies on this category — the incident is preserved and reviewed personally. This is documented practice, not marketing language.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to do if a rental partner attempts a scam</h2><p>Do not pay any off-platform or undisclosed demand. Do not surrender any additional documents or ID copies at pickup or return beyond what the booking page requires. Do not accept a demand that the vehicle be held while you make payment. If a partner is applying pressure, calmly step away and email hello@zymo.app immediately with your booking ID and 'urgent partner misconduct escalation' as the subject line. Attach photos of the pickup location and any written or WhatsApp demand you received from the partner. If a safety threat is genuine (physical intimidation, threat of violence, refusal to release the vehicle without an off-platform payment), call the police first and Zymo second — safety comes before any refund process. For non-safety scam attempts, Zymo's founder-level escalation typically results in a full refund of the trip cost plus partner review — Zymo can block a partner on the platform when misconduct is verified. Preserve every screenshot, WhatsApp message, and photo you have from the incident; these are what convert a customer's word into an actionable dispute review.</p></section>
