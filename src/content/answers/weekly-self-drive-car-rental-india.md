    <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">Can I book a self-drive car for a week in India?</h1>

    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Yes — 5-day and 7-day self-drive brackets on Zymo are popular for week-long trips and typically carry a better per-day rate than booking 7 single days. A 7-day hatchback booking typically costs about ₹6,500-9,000 in total; a 7-day SUV booking about ₹13,000-22,000 depending on city, car, and dates. Pick the duration on the booking page and the partner's actual per-day rate adjusts automatically.</p>
    
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Why a 7-day booking is cheaper per day</h2><p>Partners pad multi-day brackets with built-in discounts because longer bookings reduce their cleaning, handover, and inspection overhead. The same Swift booking might cost ₹1,500 per day on a 2-day booking but only ₹1,100 per day on a 7-day booking. The savings scale up further on 10-day, 15-day, and monthly brackets.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Common week-long use cases</h2><p>Goa or Kerala week-trip rentals from Mumbai or Bangalore. Family vacation week with airport-pickup and multiple-city itineraries. Extended Test Drives where the buyer wants a real week with the car they are evaluating. Inter-city work assignments where flying-and-renting is cheaper than driving from home.</p></section>
    <section><h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Inventory and booking lead time</h2><p>Hatchback inventory for a 7-day booking is generally available with 2-3 days lead time. Compact and Tier-1 SUVs for a 7-day booking during peak holiday windows (Diwali, Christmas, New Year, May summer-vacation) need 7-10 days lead time. Search your dates on Zymo to see live availability.</p></section>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Related questions</h2>
    <dl>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is a 7-day booking cheaper than 7 single-day bookings?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Multi-day brackets (5-day, 7-day, 10-day) carry built-in discounts. A 7-day Swift booking might average ₹1,100 per day vs ₹1,500 per day on individual single-day bookings — meaningful saving on a week-long trip.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I book a week-long Extended Test Drive?</dt>
    <dd>Yes. Extended Test Drive bookings use the same flow as a normal self-drive — pick the car and the 7-day bracket. A full week with the car you are considering buying surfaces day-five quirks (infotainment, gear-lever ergonomics, peak-traffic feel) that a 2-day weekend cannot.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is unlimited km available on weekly bookings?</dt>
    <dd>Some partners offer unlimited-km variants on 7-day bookings at a higher daily rate. For trips longer than 2,000 km over the week, the unlimited-km variant works out cheaper than paying extra-km charges on the standard booking.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">How far ahead should I book a 7-day SUV during peak season?</dt>
    <dd>7-10 days lead time for compact/Tier-1 SUVs during Diwali, Christmas, New Year, or May summer-vacation windows. Hatchback inventory for a 7-day booking is generally available with 2-3 days lead time.</dd>
    
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Can I take a 7-day booking interstate?</dt>
    <dd>Yes, subject to the partner's outstation policy. Most partners support outstation use on weekly bookings; confirm on the booking page. Inter-state surcharges and tolls are paid by you.</dd>
