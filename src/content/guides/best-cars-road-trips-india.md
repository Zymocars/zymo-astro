    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Pre-Trip Checklist for Self-Drive Road Trips in India</h2>
    <p>Before you leave the pickup point, run through this checklist. A 10-minute walk-around saves hours of trouble later.</p>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Photograph every panel.</strong> All four sides plus interior &mdash; timestamped photos protect you against pre-existing damage disputes at return.</li>
    <li><strong>Check fuel level + odometer.</strong> Match against the booking. Most policies are full-to-full; some are same-to-same.</li>
    <li><strong>Locate spare tyre, jack, jumper cables, first aid kit.</strong> Confirm presence and condition. Insist on replacements if missing.</li>
    <li><strong>Test FASTag.</strong> Stick a payment app QR sticker handy in case it deactivates mid-toll. Keep Rs.500-1,000 cash for offline tolls in remote areas.</li>
    <li><strong>Insurance + RC documents.</strong> Confirm originals are in the glovebox (legally required during traffic stops).</li>
    <li><strong>Test wipers, headlights, indicators, AC, and brakes</strong> on your first 5 km &mdash; before you&apos;re in expressway speeds.</li>
    <li><strong>Save the partner&apos;s emergency number.</strong> Roadside assistance hours and coverage area vary by partner; know what&apos;s included.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Drive defensively on Indian highways &mdash; lane discipline is loose, and ghat roads have unpredictable bus and truck behaviour. Most rental partners have a 100-110 km/h speed cap monitored by GPS; respect it to avoid penalty fees.</p>
