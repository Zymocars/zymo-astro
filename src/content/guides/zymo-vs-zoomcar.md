    <section id="model">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Aggregator vs Single-Brand Fleet</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">The first question to ask is whether you want one brand's inventory or every brand's inventory. Zymo is an aggregator &mdash; it doesn't own cars; it compares prices from multiple rental partners and shows them in one search. ZoomCar is a single-brand operator &mdash; it owns cars (and lists hosts under its brand via ZAP), but you see only ZoomCar-branded inventory in any city.</p>
    
    <p>This sounds like a small distinction. It isn't. The same Hyundai Creta in Mumbai on the same dates can have different prices across partners. With an aggregator, that price difference is visible in seconds. With a single-brand app, it isn't visible at all &mdash; you'd have to open multiple apps and compare.</p>
    
    <div style="background:#16222E;border-left:3px solid #4F7BFF;padding:12px 16px;margin:1rem 0;border-radius:0 6px 6px 0;font-size:0.9rem"><strong style="color:#4F7BFF">The flight-comparison parallel:</strong> You wouldn't book a flight without checking MakeMyTrip or Cleartrip first. Self-drive car rental works the same way. The aggregator surfaces every option in one search.</div>
    </section>
    
    <section id="cities">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">City Coverage</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Zymo operates in 60+ Indian cities, including all metros and many Tier-2 and Tier-3 destinations. ZoomCar operates in major metros and select Tier-2 cities. For booking in cities like Indore, Udupi, Mangalore, Trichy, Madurai, Mysore, Vizag, Pondicherry, Goa, Manipal, Calicut, Coimbatore, and similar growing destinations, Zymo's aggregator network typically surfaces more options because it pools inventory from multiple local partners in each city.</p>
    
    <p>For dense metro coverage with parking-zone self-service pickup, ZoomCar's purpose-built footprint in those metros is a real advantage. The two models optimise for different things: Zymo for breadth, ZoomCar for depth in the cities it serves.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section id="fleet">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Fleet and Selection</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Zymo's catalogue includes 30,000+ cars from multiple rental partners. The same car class &mdash; say, a Hyundai Creta SUV &mdash; appears multiple times in a single search, each time from a different partner with its own price, pickup point, and policy. ZoomCar shows the cars in its own fleet plus the ZAP host listings. Both models offer hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, and select MPVs in their main cities.</p>
    
    <p>The practical effect: on Zymo, a Hyundai Creta search in Mumbai might surface five or six variants of the same model from different partners at different price points. On a single-brand app, you see one Creta listing with one price for that booking window.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section id="pricing">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Pricing and Platform Fees</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Zymo's hatchbacks start from ₹45/hour in metros. Zymo charges zero platform fee &mdash; the rental price you see is the partner's price, with no marked-up Zymo fee added on top. ZoomCar's pricing is set by ZoomCar (or by the ZAP host for marketplace cars) with platform fees that vary per booking. ZoomCar typically applies a per-booking platform fee plus a security deposit.</p>
    
    <p>The structural point: an aggregator like Zymo earns commission from rental partners, not from a fee charged to you. That's why "no Zymo fee" is a verifiable structural feature, not a promotional claim. A single-brand operator that owns its fleet bundles its operating costs into the rental price plus platform fees.</p>
    
    <div style="background:#16222E;border-left:3px solid #4F7BFF;padding:12px 16px;margin:1rem 0;border-radius:0 6px 6px 0;font-size:0.9rem"><strong style="color:#4F7BFF">A note on what to compare:</strong> When comparing rental prices, always include the security deposit and platform fee in your math &mdash; not just the headline rental rate. A car at ₹1,200/day with a ₹300 platform fee plus ₹5,000 deposit is not the same as ₹1,200/day with no fee and a ₹3,000 deposit.</div>
    </section>
    
    <section id="booking">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Booking and Pickup Experience</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Both platforms support hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly bookings. ZoomCar's signature pickup model is self-service at a designated parking zone &mdash; unlock the car via the app, drive away, return to the same zone. Zymo's pickup varies by partner: some partners offer doorstep delivery, some have hub locations, some operate self-service pickup in metros. The booking page on Zymo shows each partner's specific pickup model so you can choose the option that fits your trip.</p>
    
    <p>For a 6 AM airport pickup or a midnight return after a wedding, the partner's policy &mdash; not just the brand &mdash; is what matters. The aggregator surfaces the policy alongside the price, so the decision is one screen.</p>
    </section>
    
    <section id="etd">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Extended Test Drives: A Pillar Zymo Owns</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">A short test drive isn't enough to validate a multi-lakh car purchase. Zymo pioneered the Extended Test Drive (ETD) in India &mdash; a 2&mdash;7 day self-drive booking specifically designed for people considering a car purchase. You book the exact car you're evaluating, drive it on your real daily routes for several days, and use that experience to make a buying decision. Self-drive rental and ETDs are the two pillars of what Zymo offers customers.</p>
    
    <p>ZoomCar offers self-drive rentals for trips, but doesn't position any specific product as a pre-purchase Extended Test Drive. If renting before buying is your use case, this is a structural reason to start on Zymo.</p>

    <li><strong>You're booking in a Tier-2 or Tier-3 city.</strong> 60+ cities including many destinations where single-brand operators have limited or no presence.</li>
    <li><strong>You want pickup options.</strong> Doorstep, hub, or self-service &mdash; varies by partner, all visible on the booking page.</li>
    <li><strong>You're considering buying a car.</strong> The Extended Test Drive is purpose-built for the test-before-buying decision.</li>

    <section id="choose-zoomcar">
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">When ZoomCar May Fit Better</h2>

    <li><strong>You specifically want a single-brand experience.</strong> Same brand, same support number, same colour scheme on every booking.</li>
    <li><strong>You're already a frequent ZoomCar user.</strong> Wallet credits, past trip history, and account familiarity make repeat booking quick.</li>

For most customers booking a self-drive car in 2026 &mdash; particularly outside the top metros &mdash; the aggregator surfaces more options.</p>
