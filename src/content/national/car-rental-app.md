    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Zymo is a self-drive car rental aggregator app across 60+ Indian cities. Instead of booking with a single rental company, Zymo compares prices from multiple rental partners across 60+ cities in one search — so you see their prices side by side without switching between apps. Available on Play Store and App Store, with web booking on zymo.app.</p>
    
    <p>The Indian self-drive market has multiple rental operators of varying sizes — national brands, regional fleet owners, and local providers. Each has its own app, pricing, and availability. Booking direct on a single brand&rsquo;s app means you only see that brand&rsquo;s cars and prices. An aggregator app surfaces the full market — same car category, multiple operators, real-time prices side-by-side.</p>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Why Use a Car Rental Aggregator App</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Wider inventory:</strong> cars from multiple partners in one search vs a single fleet.</li>
    <li><strong>Side-by-side price comparison:</strong> the same Hyundai Creta in Mumbai for the same dates can be priced differently across partners. The aggregator surfaces them at once.</li>
    <li><strong>Higher availability on peak dates:</strong> when one operator is sold out for your dates, others usually have inventory. One search; one outcome.</li>
    <li><strong>Transparent pricing:</strong> base fare, deposit, KM limits, and fuel policy visible upfront — across partners — without account creation on each app.</li>
    <li><strong>Unified booking + support:</strong> one Zymo account, one history, one customer support team. No juggling between brand apps.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Features That Matter in a Car Rental App</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Real-time price comparison:</strong> static prices feel outdated; the best apps show live partner pricing for your dates.</li>
    <li><strong>Filter by car type, transmission, fuel:</strong> hatchback vs SUV, manual vs automatic, petrol/diesel/electric.</li>
    <li><strong>Transparent pricing display:</strong> base + deposit + KMs + fuel policy on the listing card, before booking.</li>
    <li><strong>Aadhaar OTP verification:</strong> fast pickup handover. Indian self-drive standard.</li>
    <li><strong>Doorstep delivery:</strong> in major cities, partner delivery to your address saves time.</li>
    <li><strong>Hourly to monthly bookings:</strong> 1-hour city errand to 30-day subscription, all in one app.</li>
    <li><strong>Cancellation flexibility:</strong> partner-set; visible on booking page before payment.</li>
