    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to Check Before Booking an SUV</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Kilometre allocation:</strong> SUV trips often involve longer distances (mountain trips, road trips). A package with more included KMs avoids extra-km charges.</li>
    <li><strong>Fuel policy:</strong> SUVs use more fuel than hatchbacks. Confirm full-to-full vs same-to-same.</li>
    <li><strong>State permit + outstation policy:</strong> all-India tourist permit lets you cross state boundaries; some partners have additional outstation fees for mountain destinations.</li>
    <li><strong>Damage protection / comprehensive coverage:</strong> SUVs cost more to repair — comprehensive insurance or zero-damage protection is worth it for off-road trips.</li>
    <li><strong>4x4 vs 4x2:</strong> only relevant for Ladakh, Spiti, Rann of Kutch, off-road. Most highway trips don&rsquo;t need 4x4.</li>
    </ul>
