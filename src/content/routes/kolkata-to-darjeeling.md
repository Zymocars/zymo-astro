faqs: 
  - q: "How many days do I need for Kolkata to Darjeeling by car?"
    a: |
        Plan 5-6 days minimum. Day 1: Drive to Siliguri (10 hrs, stay overnight). Day 2: Drive to Darjeeling (2 hrs), explore. Day 3-4: Tiger Hill sunrise, tea gardens, Batasia Loop. Day 5: Drive back via Siliguri. Breaking the drive is important — 12 hours straight is exhausting.
  - q: "Is the mountain road to Darjeeling safe?"
    a: |
        Yes, but requires careful driving. The 80 km mountain stretch from Siliguri to Darjeeling has sharp switchbacks and narrow sections. Drive in daylight only. An SUV handles the gradients better. The road is paved but can be foggy in monsoon.
  - q: "How many tolls from Kolkata to Darjeeling?"
    a: |
        There are approximately 2 toll plazas on the Kolkata to Darjeeling route, with a combined cost of around ₹165 one-way for cars. Tolls in order: NH34 tolls (₹120), Siliguri approach (₹45). Toll rates revise periodically; carry a FASTag for cashless payments.
  - q: "How much toll from Kolkata to Darjeeling?"
    a: |
        Total toll charges from Kolkata to Darjeeling are approximately ₹165 one-way for a private car, spread across 2 toll plazas. With FASTag, payment is automatic. For a round trip, double the toll cost. Rates revise periodically.
  - q: "How long is the drive from Kolkata to Darjeeling?"
    a: |
        The drive from Kolkata to Darjeeling takes approximately 12 hours for a distance of 600 km. Actual time depends on traffic, weather, and stops. Plan an early start to avoid peak-hour traffic near both cities and to reach before sunset on highway sections.
  - q: "How far is Darjeeling from Kolkata by car?"
    a: |
        Darjeeling is approximately 600 km from Kolkata by road. The drive takes around 12 hours via the standard route. Distance varies slightly by route choice (highway vs scenic), but 600 km is the typical figure for navigation apps.
