  <h1 style="color:#FFFFFF;font-size:1.6rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem">About Zymo &mdash; India&rsquo;s Self-Drive Car Rental Aggregator</h1>
  
  <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Zymo is a self-drive car rental aggregator across 60+ Indian cities, comparing cars from multiple partners, so a single search shows you side-by-side prices, car options, and pickup points from operators that would otherwise need separate apps and accounts. Founded in 2023, Zymo charges no platform fee on top of the partner&rsquo;s rate.</p>
  
  <section>
  <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.3rem">What Zymo Does</h2>
  <p>Self-drive car rental in India is fragmented. Different operators serve different cities, list different car categories, and price the same vehicle differently. Zymo aggregates the supply &mdash; you pick a city, dates, and car type, and we show the booking options from the partners that serve that route, with the price each one is charging. You compare. You book. You drive.</p>
  
  <p>This works for two distinct use cases:</p>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>Self-drive bookings for trips</strong> &mdash; weekend getaways, outstation drives, airport pickups, monthly subscriptions, business travel.</li>
  <li><strong>Extended Test Drives</strong> &mdash; self-drive bookings of one day to a month, designed for people considering a car purchase. Drive the model you want to buy on your real daily routes before committing. <a href="/guides/rent-before-you-buy" style="color:#4F7BFF;text-decoration:none">Read the Extended Test Drive guide</a>.</li>
  </ul>
  </section>
  
  <section>
  <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.3rem">How Zymo Compares to Booking Direct</h2>
  <p>Without Zymo, finding the best self-drive option in your city means installing several different apps, creating accounts on each, uploading documents to each, and manually comparing prices. With Zymo, that&rsquo;s a single search. Zymo also adds Aadhaar OTP verification (which means faster pickup), a unified support channel for any partner-specific issue, and consistent cancellation handling across operators.</p>
