  <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.3rem">Charging an EV During Your Rental</h2>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>Home or hotel charging (slow):</strong> Plug into any 15A socket overnight. Full charge in 8&ndash;10 hours. Most rental EVs include a home charging cable. Ideal for overnight stays.</li>
  <li><strong>Public fast charging:</strong> Get to 80% in 60&ndash;90 minutes at fast chargers. Available at malls, highway stops, and commercial areas. Apps like Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq, and Ather Grid help you find nearby stations.</li>
  <li><strong>Range for city use:</strong> Most EVs offer 250&ndash;400 km. For typical city driving of 40&ndash;60 km/day, a single charge lasts 4&ndash;7 days. Range anxiety is mostly a non-issue in cities.</li>
  <li><strong>Highway trip planning:</strong> For 200+ km trips, plan one charging stop. Major highways (Delhi-Agra, Bangalore-Mysore, Mumbai-Pune) have good charger coverage. Keep a 20% battery buffer.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.3rem">First Time Renting an EV? Quick Tips</h2>
  <ol>
  <li>Download a charging app (Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq) before pickup so you know where chargers are.</li>
  <li>EVs have instant torque &mdash; they accelerate faster than petrol cars. Take it easy on the accelerator initially.</li>
  <li>Regenerative braking feels different &mdash; the car slows when you lift off the accelerator. You&rsquo;ll get used to it quickly.</li>
  <li>AC usage reduces range by 10&ndash;15%. On hot days, pre-cool the car while it&rsquo;s still plugged in.</li>
  <li>For highway trips over 200 km, plan your charging stops in advance and keep a 20% battery buffer.</li>
  <li>No special licence needed &mdash; EVs drive like any automatic car. If you&rsquo;ve driven automatic before, you&rsquo;re ready.</li>
