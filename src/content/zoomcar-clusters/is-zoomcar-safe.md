ZoomCar is a legitimate self-drive car rental platform that has operated in India since 2013. All cars on ZoomCar carry mandatory third-party insurance under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988. Before booking, verify the comprehensive insurance coverage level listed on each car, check the breakdown assistance policy, and photograph the car at pickup to document pre-existing condition.</p>

    <li><strong>Mandatory insurance:</strong> third-party cover is required by law on every ZoomCar vehicle</li>

    <li><strong>Pickup inspection:</strong> photograph the car from all angles and note pre-existing damage on the handover form</li>
    <li><strong>Emergency support:</strong> check the ZoomCar app and your booking confirmation for its emergency support options</li>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to verify before a ZoomCar booking</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Insurance coverage level</strong> — third-party is standard; check if comprehensive (own-damage, theft) is included or an add-on</li>
    <li><strong>Deductible / excess amount</strong> — the amount you pay in case of an at-fault incident. Varies by car class.</li>
    <li><strong>Fuel policy</strong> — full-to-full (you return with same fuel as pickup) or fixed-fuel (included in price)</li>
    <li><strong>KM limit</strong> — extra charges apply above the included daily/hourly kilometre allowance</li>
    <li><strong>Pickup zone</strong> — ZoomCar often uses hub pickup rather than doorstep delivery; confirm the exact address</li>
    <li><strong>Cancellation terms</strong> — how far in advance you can cancel without penalty</li>
    </ol>

    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Safe pickup checklist for any self-drive rental (including ZoomCar)</h2>
    <ul>
    <li>Photograph the car from front, back, both sides, roof, and interior dashboard before driving away</li>
    <li>Check the fuel gauge and confirm it matches the booking document</li>
    <li>Note the odometer reading at pickup</li>
    <li>Check tyre tread and tyre pressure visually</li>
    <li>Confirm insurance documents and the car's RC (Registration Certificate) are in the glovebox</li>
    <li>Save the partner's emergency contact before leaving the pickup point</li>
    </ul>

    <dd>Check the ZoomCar app for its roadside-assistance terms. Coverage and response time vary by city and route, so for long-distance trips confirm the coverage area before booking.</dd>
    <dt style="color:#FFFFFF;font-weight:600;margin-top:1rem">Is ZoomCar insurance enough or do I need extra cover?</dt>
    <dd>Third-party insurance is mandatory and covers damage or injury to others. If the listing's comprehensive cover has a high deductible (₹10,000+), consider whether you want an upgrade. The booking page on ZoomCar or on Zymo shows the specific deductible amount before payment.</dd>

Route planning, fuel stops, and overnight stays on long routes require the same preparation as any solo drive.</dd>
