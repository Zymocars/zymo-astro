    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What is ZoomCar's damage policy?</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar bookings include Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) as standard. CDW caps your liability for damage — meaning that even if you are involved in an accident, you pay only the deductible amount, not the full repair cost. The deductible varies by car class and is shown on the booking page before you confirm. All ZoomCar cars also carry mandatory third-party insurance under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988.</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What does CDW cover?</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Covered:</strong> collision damage, accidental body damage, dents from accidents during normal driving</li>
    <li><strong>Covered:</strong> third-party property damage (handled by the mandatory third-party insurance)</li>
    <li><strong>Not covered:</strong> damage due to driving under the influence (DUI) — ZoomCar voids CDW if alcohol is detected</li>
    <li><strong>Not covered:</strong> damage from driving on non-permitted roads (river crossings, restricted terrain)</li>
    <li><strong>Not covered:</strong> tyre punctures, windshield chips from road debris in some listing terms — verify the listing's specific terms</li>
    <li><strong>Not covered:</strong> interior damage (cuts, burns, spills on seats) — this is separate from CDW and comes out of the security deposit</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What counts as damage at ZoomCar?</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar's post-trip inspection checks the exterior panels, glass, tyres, and interior. Anything that was not documented in the pre-trip inspection and is found at return will be assessed for cost. Even minor scratches and small dents count — the threshold is lower than you might expect. This is why pre-trip photographic documentation is critical.</p>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Pre-trip documentation checklist</h2>
    <ol>
    <li>Open the ZoomCar app and start the pre-trip inspection before touching the car</li>
    <li>Photograph every exterior panel: front bumper, rear bumper, all four door panels, bonnet, boot lid, roof</li>
    <li>Photograph all four tyres including sidewalls — note any existing cuts or bulges</li>
    <li>Photograph the windshield for any existing chips or cracks</li>
    <li>Photograph the interior: seats, dashboard, carpets, door panels</li>
    <li>Capture the fuel gauge and odometer in the same photo for a timestamp</li>
    <li>Submit the pre-trip inspection inside the app — this timestamps all photos in ZoomCar's system</li>
    </ol>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to dispute a ZoomCar damage charge</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Gather evidence immediately:</strong> your pre-trip inspection photos (timestamped in the app), photos taken at drop-off, any photos from during the trip</li>
    <li><strong>Contact ZoomCar support</strong> within 24 hours of the trip end via in-app chat — include booking ID and describe the dispute clearly</li>
    <li><strong>Email ZoomCar</strong> with all evidence attached — photos, booking ID, a factual written account. Use the contact details on your booking confirmation</li>
    <li><strong>Escalate to Zymo</strong> if you booked through Zymo — Zymo's partner relations team can assist in dispute escalation with ZoomCar</li>
    <li><strong>Consumer forum</strong> if unresolved after 21 days — file with the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000) or through the eDaakhil consumer forum portal</li>
    <li><strong>Chargeback</strong> as a last resort if the charge was applied without adequate justification — contact your card-issuing bank with evidence</li>
    </ol>
