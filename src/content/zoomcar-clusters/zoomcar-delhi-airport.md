    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How does ZoomCar pickup work at Delhi IGI airport?</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar uses designated hub zones at Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport (IGI). The car is not driven to your terminal exit — instead, it is parked at a hub zone within the airport complex. Your booking confirmation and the ZoomCar app show the exact zone location including the terminal and parking level. T1 (domestic) and T3 (international) are in different parts of the IGI complex, so confirm which terminal you are arriving at before travelling to the zone. Use the ZoomCar app to unlock the car after completing the in-app pre-trip inspection.</p>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Step-by-step pickup procedure at IGI</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Find the hub zone:</strong> open the ZoomCar app and check your active booking for the exact zone location, parking level, and bay number at IGI.</li>
    <li><strong>Walk to the pickup zone:</strong> follow airport signage for the car park. T1 and T3 are separate — T1 serves domestic routes, T3 handles international and major domestic carriers. Confirm in the app before leaving your terminal.</li>
    <li><strong>Check car photos match the listing:</strong> visually confirm the registration plate against the booking before unlocking.</li>
    <li><strong>Complete the pre-trip inspection in app:</strong> photograph all four sides, the dashboard odometer, and the fuel gauge. Timestamps are stored automatically.</li>
    <li><strong>Note fuel level and odometer reading:</strong> screenshot both from the app at trip start.</li>
    <li><strong>Start trip:</strong> tap "Start Trip" in the app to unlock and begin your booking timer.</li>
    </ol>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to do if the car is not at the IGI pickup zone</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Do not leave the zone.</strong> Stay until you have documented the absence and received a response from ZoomCar support.</li>
    <li><strong>Use in-app SOS immediately</strong> after waiting a few minutes. Open ZoomCar app → active booking → SOS.</li>
    <li><strong>Document with a timestamped photo</strong> of the empty parking bay from your phone — location metadata supports your claim.</li>
    <li><strong>Request a refund or replacement:</strong> ask ZoomCar support for a replacement car or a refund in the SOS chat.</li>
    </ol>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Return procedure at Delhi IGI airport</h2>
    <ol>
    <li>Return the car to the same hub zone (or a confirmed alternate zone for one-way bookings).</li>
    <li>Complete the post-trip inspection in the ZoomCar app — photograph all sides and the fuel gauge.</li>
    <li>Lock via the app and tap "End Trip." Wait for the in-app confirmation.</li>
    <li>Screenshot the final odometer and fuel reading for your records.</li>
    </ol>
