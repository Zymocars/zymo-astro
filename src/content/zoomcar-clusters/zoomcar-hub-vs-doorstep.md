    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How hub pickup works</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">With hub pickup, you go to a designated ZoomCar hub — such as an airport, metro station, or fixed city zone — collect the car, do a pre-trip inspection, and drive away. Hubs are fixed, mapped locations shown in the app.</p>
    <p>For many bookings — especially those starting or ending at an airport — hub pickup is the most straightforward option. Check the ZoomCar app for the hub locations in your city.</p>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How doorstep delivery works — and what it costs</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Doorstep delivery means the car is brought to your location — home, office, or hotel — where available. Any delivery charge is shown on the listing and at checkout. Availability varies by city and zone.</p>
    <p>Doorstep delivery is most useful for early morning departures where getting to a hub at 5am or 6am is inconvenient, for users who are unfamiliar with the hub location in an unfamiliar city, and for bookings where the convenience premium is worth avoiding the transit to a hub. For routine bookings where hub access is easy, the delivery charge may not be worth it.</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to choose — use-case guide</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Choose hub pickup when you are starting your trip from an airport with a hub, when you are in a city you know well and the hub is nearby, or when you want to minimise total booking cost. Choose doorstep delivery when your departure time is before 7am and reaching a hub is impractical, when you are in an unfamiliar city and want the car brought to your hotel, or when the delivery charge is worth the saved logistics time for your trip. At Zymo checkout, the options available for your booking are shown.</p>
