    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How hub pickup works</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Hub pickup is ZoomCar's standard delivery model. You go to a designated ZoomCar hub — typically at an airport, metro station, or fixed city zone — collect the car, do a pre-trip inspection, and drive away. The hub model keeps ZoomCar's base pricing lower because there are no delivery logistics to account for. Hubs are fixed, well-mapped locations and the car condition is documented at a single known point rather than wherever delivery happens to occur.</p>
    <p>For most bookings — especially those starting or ending at an airport — hub pickup is the most straightforward option. ZoomCar hubs at major airports (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Goa, Kochi, Kolkata, Jaipur) are the most reliable pickup points in those cities. In city zones, hubs are at metro stations and fixed parking zones — easy to locate via the ZoomCar app on Zymo at checkout.</p>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How doorstep delivery works — and what it costs</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Doorstep delivery means ZoomCar brings the car to your location — home, office, or hotel. This adds a convenience premium of approximately ₹200–₹500 per booking depending on the city and delivery zone. Doorstep availability varies — it is not offered in all cities or all zones within a city. The car is delivered by a ZoomCar staff member who walks you through the pre-trip inspection at your location before handing over the keys.</p>
    <p>Doorstep delivery is most useful for early morning departures where getting to a hub at 5am or 6am is inconvenient, for users who are unfamiliar with the hub location in an unfamiliar city, and for bookings where the convenience premium is worth avoiding the transit to a hub. For most routine bookings where hub access is easy, the additional ₹200–₹500 charge is typically not worth it.</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to choose — use-case guide</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Choose hub pickup when you are starting your trip from an airport (ZoomCar hubs at all major airports), when you are in a city you know well and the hub is nearby, or when you want to minimise total booking cost. Choose doorstep delivery when your departure time is before 7am and reaching a hub is impractical, when you are in an unfamiliar city and want the car brought to your hotel, or when the ₹200–₹500 premium is worth the saved logistics time for your specific trip. At Zymo checkout, both options are shown where available — the hub option is pre-selected as the default since it is available in every city. Zip (Zymo's own fleet) operates primarily through hub and designated zone pickup in the cities where it runs, similar to ZoomCar's hub model.</p>

    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Is ZoomCar doorstep delivery available in all cities?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">No. Doorstep delivery is available in select cities and specific delivery zones within those cities. Hub pickup is available across all ZoomCar cities. At Zymo checkout, the available delivery options are shown for your specific location and booking date.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">How much does ZoomCar doorstep delivery cost?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Doorstep delivery typically adds ₹200–₹500 to the booking cost depending on the city and delivery distance. The exact charge is shown at checkout before you confirm the booking.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Can I return a ZoomCar to a different location than pickup?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">One-way returns (different hub or zone from pickup) are available on some ZoomCar routes — particularly city-to-city routes like Mumbai to Pune or Bangalore to Mysore. One-way options are shown at checkout when available. For standard bookings, return to the same hub is the default.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Where are ZoomCar hubs located at airports?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar operates hub zones at major Indian airports including Mumbai (CSIA T1/T2), Delhi (IGI T1/T3), Bangalore (BLR Kempegowda), Hyderabad (Rajiv Gandhi International), Chennai (MAA), Pune (Lohegaon), Goa (Dabolim), Kochi (Nedumbassery), Kolkata (NSCBI), and Jaipur (Sanganer). Hub zone instructions are shown in the ZoomCar app at booking confirmation.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Is hub pickup or doorstep delivery better for an early morning flight?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">For an early morning return flight, doorstep delivery for pickup is worth considering if reaching the hub before 6am is difficult. For a morning departure on a road trip, hub pickup at the airport is usually the most efficient — you pick up the car as you land and drive directly out. Doorstep adds value when you are starting the trip from home or hotel at an early hour.</p>
