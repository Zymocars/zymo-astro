    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How does ZoomCar airport pickup work at Mumbai?</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar uses a hub-model pickup at Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (CSIA). Unlike doorstep delivery, the car is parked at a designated hub zone near the airport premises — not driven to your terminal exit. Your booking confirmation and the ZoomCar app show the exact hub zone location. Arrive at the zone, unlock the car via the app, and complete the in-app pre-trip inspection before driving away. The pickup zone for T1 (domestic) and T2 (international) are in different parts of the airport complex — confirm which terminal you are arriving at before travelling to the zone.</p>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Step-by-step pickup procedure at CSIA</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Find the hub zone:</strong> open the ZoomCar app and navigate to your active booking. The hub zone location and parking bay details are shown on the booking screen. Note the zone before you leave the terminal.</li>
    <li><strong>Walk to the pickup zone:</strong> follow the airport signage for the designated parking zone. For T1 departures and arrivals, the zone is accessible on the ground floor. For T2 international arrivals, follow the parking area signs after exiting customs.</li>
    <li><strong>Check car photos match the listing:</strong> before unlocking, visually confirm the car registration plate matches the booking. ZoomCar apps shows the car number.</li>
    <li><strong>Complete the pre-trip inspection in app:</strong> photograph all four sides, the dashboard odometer, and the fuel gauge. These photos are timestamped and stored — they protect you against any pre-existing damage disputes.</li>
    <li><strong>Note fuel level and odometer reading:</strong> screenshot the fuel and odometer from inside the app at the start of your trip.</li>
    <li><strong>Start trip:</strong> tap "Start Trip" in the app to unlock the car and begin your booking timer.</li>
    </ol>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to do if the car is not at the pickup zone</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Do not leave the zone area.</strong> Leaving before documenting absence weakens your claim for a refund or replacement.</li>
    <li><strong>Use in-app SOS immediately</strong> after waiting a few minutes. Open ZoomCar app → active booking → SOS. Describe the situation: "Car not present at hub zone for booking ID [YYY]."</li>
    <li><strong>Document with a timestamped photo:</strong> photograph the empty parking bay and the surroundings. The timestamp and location metadata on the photo serve as evidence.</li>
    <li><strong>Request a refund or replacement:</strong> ask ZoomCar support for a replacement car or a refund in the SOS chat.</li>
    </ol>
    </section>
    
    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Return procedure at Mumbai airport</h2>
    <ol>
    <li>Drive back to the same hub zone where you picked up the car (or an alternate zone confirmed in the app for one-way bookings).</li>
    <li>Complete the post-trip inspection in the ZoomCar app — photograph all sides and the fuel gauge to document return condition.</li>
    <li>Lock the car via the app and tap "End Trip." Wait for the in-app confirmation before leaving.</li>
    <li>Screenshot the final odometer and fuel reading for your records.</li>
    </ol>
