    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Interstate travel with a ZoomCar — rules and documents</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Documents in the car:</strong> check that the RC (registration certificate), valid insurance policy, and Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate are in the glovebox before you leave. They are required at state border checkpoints.</li>
    <li><strong>Trips outside the pickup city:</strong> check the booking policy or in-app terms for any rule on taking the car outside the pickup city.</li>
    <li><strong>Interstate permits:</strong> for a privately registered car, no tourist permit or inter-state permit is required for private car travel across most state borders.</li>
    <li><strong>Exceptions:</strong> Jammu and Kashmir and some northeastern states require a local permit for vehicles registered in other states. Check state-specific entry rules before planning.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Toll payment on outstation ZoomCar trips</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Toll charges on outstation ZoomCar trips are your responsibility as the driver. Confirm before departure that the car has a FASTag by checking the windscreen for the sticker. If the FASTag balance is low, recharge it via the NHAI FASTag portal, Paytm, or your bank's FASTag app using the tag ID (visible on the sticker). Carry cash as a backup for older toll plazas on state highways and minor national highways that may not have electronic toll gates.</p>
