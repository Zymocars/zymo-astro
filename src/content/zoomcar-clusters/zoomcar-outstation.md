    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Can you take a ZoomCar on an outstation trip?</h2>
    <p data-speakable="true" style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Yes. ZoomCar cars can be taken on outstation trips. For long outstation journeys, ZoomCar's Subscription plan (7-day minimum, unlimited KM) is the most cost-effective option. For shorter outstation trips of 1–3 days, standard daily-rate bookings work — but verify the included KM limit carefully. Extra-km charges on a 600 km round trip can be significant on a capped daily-rate plan. ZoomCar cars come with the RC, insurance certificate, and pollution certificate in the car — these are required at interstate checkpoints.</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Interstate travel with a ZoomCar — rules and documents</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Documents in the car:</strong> all ZoomCar cars carry the RC (registration certificate), valid insurance policy, and Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate in the glovebox. These are required at state border checkpoints.</li>
    <li><strong>Inform ZoomCar for trips outside the metro zone:</strong> ZoomCar's policy requires you to notify them before taking the car outside the listed pickup city's metropolitan area. Check the specific booking policy or in-app terms for your car.</li>
    <li><strong>Interstate permits are not required for private cars:</strong> ZoomCar cars are registered as private vehicles (not commercial taxis). No tourist permit or inter-state permit is required for private car travel across most state borders.</li>
    <li><strong>Exceptions:</strong> Jammu and Kashmir and some northeastern states require a local permit for vehicles registered in other states. Check state-specific entry rules before planning.</li>
    </ul>

    <section>
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Toll payment on outstation ZoomCar trips</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Toll charges on outstation ZoomCar trips are your responsibility as the driver. Most ZoomCar cars have FASTag installed — confirm before departure by checking the windscreen for the FASTag sticker. If the FASTag balance is low, recharge it via the NHAI FASTag portal, Paytm, or your bank's FASTag app using the tag ID (visible on the sticker). Carry cash as a backup for older toll plazas on state highways and minor national highways that may not have electronic toll gates.</p>
