    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What causes a deposit deduction?</h2>
    <ul>
    <li><strong>Damage to the vehicle:</strong> any damage not documented at pickup — scratches, dents, cracked glass, interior stains — can result in deduction up to the damage repair cost</li>
    <li><strong>Fuel not refilled:</strong> if the car is returned with less fuel than at pickup and your booking has a fuel-return clause, the cost difference is deducted</li>
    <li><strong>Toll or traffic violations:</strong> toll charges or traffic fines incurred during the trip that were not paid separately</li>
    <li><strong>Exceeding kilometre limits:</strong> charges for extra kilometres may be deducted from the deposit if not settled separately</li>
    <li><strong>Late return:</strong> if the car is returned beyond the agreed time window without prior extension approval</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How to protect your deposit before pickup</h2>
    <ul>
    <li>Photograph every panel of the car — front, rear, both sides, roof, all four tyres — before starting the trip inside the ZoomCar app's pre-trip inspection</li>
    <li>Complete the in-app pre-trip inspection; this timestamps your photos inside ZoomCar's records and is the strongest protection if a damage dispute arises later</li>
    <li>Note the fuel gauge reading in photos so you can match it at return</li>
    <li>Check for pre-existing interior wear (seat tears, dashboard marks) and flag them in the app before driving away</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What to do if your ZoomCar deposit refund is delayed</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Check the refund timeline</strong> — for bookings through Zymo the deposit is refunded within 5–7 working days after the trip; for direct ZoomCar bookings the app shows the timeline</li>
    <li><strong>Check the ZoomCar app</strong> — confirm the booking shows as closed</li>
    <li><strong>Contact ZoomCar support</strong> via in-app chat with your booking ID and the date the trip was closed</li>
    <li><strong>Email ZoomCar</strong> if in-app support is slow — include booking ID, trip end date, and the payment method used</li>
    <li><strong>Escalate through Zymo</strong> if you booked via Zymo — email hello@zymo.app with your booking ID and Zymo follows up with the partner</li>
    <li><strong>Consumer forum / chargeback</strong> if there is still no resolution — file with the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000) or initiate a chargeback through your bank or card provider</li>
    </ol>
