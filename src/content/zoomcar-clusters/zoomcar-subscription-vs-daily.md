    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How ZoomCar's two plans work</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar operates two distinct pricing structures. The standard daily rental charges a flat rate per day plus a per-km charge beyond the included daily allowance — typically 100–150km/day, with extra km charged at ₹8–₹12/km. This is the right structure for occasional use: a weekend trip, a single-day drive, or a short outstation run where you know you will stay within the km cap.</p>
    <p>ZoomCar's Subscription plan is a multi-day rental (minimum 7 days) with unlimited km included in the flat daily rate. No per-km overage. The daily rate under Subscription is higher than standard daily, but the unlimited km makes it economical for long-distance travel. Monthly Subscription pricing runs approximately ₹18,000–₹25,000/month for hatchbacks and compact SUVs, varying by city and car class. This is designed for road trips, weekly commuters, and anyone driving 1,500km or more in a month.</p>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Breakeven analysis — which plan wins at what distance</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">The crossover point where Subscription becomes cheaper than daily rental falls at roughly 1,500km per month (or the equivalent per booking period). Below that threshold, standard daily rental is more economical — you pay only for the km you actually drive. Above 1,500km/month, the Subscription flat rate is lower than the standard daily rate plus accumulated per-km charges. For a 7-day road trip covering 2,000km, Subscription saves you the per-km charges on all distance beyond the daily cap — at ₹10/km that is ₹500–₹1,000 per day in savings at scale.</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Which plan for outstation trips?</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">For a weekend outstation (2 days, ~300–400km round trip), standard daily rental is the better choice — the km cap of 150–200km per day typically covers the distance and you avoid the 7-day minimum commitment of Subscription. For multi-day outstation drives (Delhi to Manali, Mumbai to Goa, a Rajasthan circuit), Subscription is significantly more economical once you cross 500km in a 3-day booking. The math is straightforward: at ₹10/km for 300km of extra distance, you pay ₹3,000 in overages on a standard booking vs zero on Subscription.</p>

    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">What is ZoomCar Subscription?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar Subscription is a multi-day rental plan with unlimited km included in a flat daily rate. The minimum booking duration is 7 days. It is designed for extended use — outstation road trips, weekly commuters, or anyone who drives more than 1,500km in a month. No per-km charges apply beyond the flat rate.</p>

Compare current Subscription rates on Zymo for your city and preferred car type — pricing varies with availability.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Is ZoomCar Subscription worth it for a weekend trip?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">For a weekend trip (2 days), standard daily rental is more economical — you avoid the 7-day minimum commitment. Subscription makes sense when you are booking 7 or more days and expect to drive significantly more than the daily km cap. For a weekend trip of 300–400km, standard daily rental with the included km cap typically covers the distance without hitting overage charges.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">What is the breakeven between ZoomCar daily and Subscription?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">The breakeven falls at roughly 1,500km per month of total driving. Below this, daily rental is cheaper because you pay per-km only on overages. Above 1,500km/month, Subscription's flat rate undercuts daily rental plus accumulated overage charges. For a 7-day booking covering 2,000km, Subscription savings are typically ₹3,000–₹7,000 in avoided overage charges.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Can I switch from daily rental to Subscription mid-booking?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar's pricing plan is set at booking time — you cannot switch from standard daily to Subscription partway through a trip. If you anticipate driving significantly more than the daily km cap, book Subscription from the start. If you are unsure, compare the total estimated cost (daily rate + expected overage km × per-km rate) against the Subscription total before confirming.</p>
