    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Car not at pickup — what to do</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Check the ZoomCar app first</strong> — confirm the exact pickup hub location; some hubs have changed or been updated after booking confirmation</li>
    <li><strong>Wait 10–15 minutes</strong> then use the in-app SOS immediately — do not wait longer than 15 minutes without escalating</li>
    <li><strong>Document everything:</strong> screenshot the booking, photograph the empty pickup location with a timestamp</li>
    <li><strong>Request a replacement car or full refund</strong> via SOS — if the car is confirmed unavailable, ZoomCar's policy is to provide an equivalent replacement or a full refund</li>
    <li><strong>Contact Zymo support</strong> if you booked through Zymo — Zymo can escalate to ZoomCar's partner relations team on your behalf, which typically results in faster resolution than consumer-facing support</li>
    </ol>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Damage dispute process</h2>
    <ol>
    <li>Gather all pre-trip inspection photos taken inside the ZoomCar app (these are timestamped and stored in ZoomCar's system)</li>
    <li>Contact ZoomCar in-app chat within 24 hours of trip end — state clearly: "I am disputing damage charge of ₹X on booking ID [YYY]. Photos from pre-trip inspection show the damage was pre-existing."</li>
    <li>Follow up by email with all photos attached and a written factual account</li>
    <li>If unresolved in 7 days, escalate to the consumer forum or initiate a chargeback through your bank</li>
    </ol>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Refund not received — escalation path</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar processes refunds within 5–7 working days of trip close. If the refund has not arrived after 10 working days, follow this escalation path:</p>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>In-app chat:</strong> raise a refund query with your booking ID and trip close date</li>
    <li><strong>Email ZoomCar support:</strong> include booking ID, payment method, and date the trip was closed</li>
    <li><strong>Zymo escalation:</strong> if booked via Zymo, contact Zymo support — Zymo can follow up through ZoomCar's partner channel</li>
    <li><strong>Consumer forum:</strong> file a complaint with the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000) or eDaakhil portal after 21 days without resolution</li>
    <li><strong>Chargeback:</strong> if the refund is clearly overdue with no response, your card issuer can initiate a chargeback as a last resort</li>
    </ol>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">How Zymo helps when ZoomCar support is slow</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">If you booked a ZoomCar through Zymo, Zymo has a dedicated partner relations channel with ZoomCar. This means Zymo can escalate on your behalf when consumer-facing support queues are slow. Contact Zymo support with your booking ID, describe the issue, and Zymo will follow up through the partner channel. This is a direct benefit of booking through an aggregator versus directly on ZoomCar.</p>
