    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Car not at pickup — what to do</h2>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>Check the ZoomCar app first</strong> — confirm the exact pickup hub location; some hubs have changed or been updated after booking confirmation</li>
    <li><strong>Wait a few minutes</strong> then use the in-app SOS — do not wait long without escalating</li>
    <li><strong>Document everything:</strong> screenshot the booking, photograph the empty pickup location with a timestamp</li>
    <li><strong>Request a replacement car or a refund</strong> — ask ZoomCar support via SOS</li>
    <li><strong>Contact Zymo</strong> if you booked through Zymo — email hello@zymo.app with your booking ID and Zymo follows up with the partner</li>
    </ol>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Damage dispute process</h2>
    <ol>
    <li>Gather all pre-trip inspection photos taken inside the ZoomCar app (these are timestamped and stored in ZoomCar's system)</li>
    <li>Contact ZoomCar in-app chat as soon as possible after the trip (the dispute window is shown in the app) — state clearly: "I am disputing damage charge of ₹X on booking ID [YYY]. Photos from pre-trip inspection show the damage was pre-existing."</li>
    <li>Follow up by email with all photos attached and a written factual account</li>
    <li>If it stays unresolved, escalate to the consumer forum or initiate a chargeback through your bank</li>
    </ol>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Refund not received — escalation path</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">The ZoomCar app shows the refund timeline for your booking. If the refund has not arrived when expected, follow this escalation path:</p>
    <ol>
    <li><strong>In-app chat:</strong> raise a refund query with your booking ID and trip close date</li>
    <li><strong>Email ZoomCar support:</strong> include booking ID, payment method, and date the trip was closed</li>
    <li><strong>Zymo escalation:</strong> if booked via Zymo, email hello@zymo.app with your booking ID and Zymo follows up with the partner</li>
    <li><strong>Consumer forum:</strong> if it stays unresolved, file a complaint with the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000) or the eDaakhil portal</li>
    <li><strong>Chargeback:</strong> if the refund is clearly overdue with no response, your card issuer can initiate a chargeback as a last resort</li>
    </ol>
