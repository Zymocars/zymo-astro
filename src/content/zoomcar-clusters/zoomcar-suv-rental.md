    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Compact SUVs — family outstation and hill stations</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">The Creta AT, Nexon AMT, and Brezza AT cover the vast majority of family outstation trips — weekend breaks to Coorg, Ooty, Munnar, Mahabaleshwar, or Kasol. Ground clearance is adequate for paved hill roads and mild village kutcha roads. Automatic transmission makes mountain driving less fatiguing. These also fall under the 21+ age requirement, making them accessible to a wider range of drivers.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Full-size SUVs — Ladakh, Spiti, and rough terrain</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">For Ladakh, Spiti, Zanskar, or Arunachal Pradesh, the Fortuner (221mm, 4x4, diesel) and Thar (226mm, 4WD off-road specialist) are the practical choices. The XUV700 with 4WD is a strong highway option with ADAS features, but its 200mm clearance is not rated for sustained off-road use the way the Fortuner and Thar are. Note that full-size SUVs require the driver to be 23+.</p>
