    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">The core difference: self-drive vs chauffeured</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">A cab (Ola, Uber, Rapido) puts a driver in the car with you. A ZoomCar booking gives you the vehicle entirely — you drive, you decide the route, you stop where you want, and no one is watching the meter. These are genuinely different products for different situations. The decision comes down to distance, privacy, and how much of the day you need the vehicle.</p>
    <p>For city travel under 40–50km, a cab is usually simpler: no pickup logistics, no fuel responsibility, no parking to manage. For full-day trips, road trips, or situations where privacy matters, self-drive consistently wins on cost and flexibility once you cross the breakeven distance.</p>
    <p>ZoomCar pricing is typically a flat daily rate plus a per-km charge beyond the daily allowance. Cab pricing is entirely per-km plus surge. The breakeven point — where ZoomCar becomes cheaper than a cab — typically falls between 80km and 120km of driving in a single day, depending on cab surge conditions and the car class you choose on ZoomCar.</p>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost comparison — where the breakeven falls</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">A standard hatchback on ZoomCar runs approximately ₹700–₹1,100/day for a base booking with 100–150km included. Ola/Uber per-km rates for a mini or sedan are typically ₹10–₹16/km in metros, with surge during peak hours. For a 100km day of driving, a cab at ₹13/km costs ₹1,300, against a ZoomCar hatchback booking at ₹800 — a ₹500 difference before surge. The gap widens significantly on weekend evenings, mornings, or during rain when cab surge can reach 1.5x–2.5x base rates.</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">When a cab is the better choice</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Cabs make more sense in specific scenarios. Airport drop or pickup where you need to be dropped at the terminal and not worry about parking or vehicle return — a cab eliminates all logistics. Heavily congested metro zones (South Mumbai, central Delhi, Bangalore MG Road corridor) where parking is scarce or expensive and you may only need to cover 20–30km — the per-km cost stays low and you don't need to manage the vehicle. Situations where you don't have a valid driving licence or have consumed alcohol — cabs are the only responsible option. Short one-way trips within a city where returning a ZoomCar to its hub would cost you extra time and distance.</p>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">When self-drive wins — and how Zymo helps you compare</h2>

Zymo shows both ZoomCar and Zip options in a single search — compare prices, km allowances, and pickup locations before booking. Zip is Zymo's own fleet: typically strong inventory in key cities with competitive daily rates alongside ZoomCar's wider network.</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Frequently asked questions</h2>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Is ZoomCar cheaper than Ola for a full day?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">For driving 100km or more in a day, ZoomCar is typically cheaper than Ola. A hatchback ZoomCar booking at ₹800–₹1,100/day compares favourably to Ola at ₹10–₹16/km for the same distance, especially if Ola surge applies. Below 60–70km, Ola is simpler and may be cheaper once you account for ZoomCar's fuel-refill responsibility.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Do I need a driving licence for ZoomCar?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Yes. ZoomCar requires a valid Indian driving licence (LMV class) and you must be at least 21. Aadhaar OTP verification on Zymo confirms your identity quickly. If you don't have a licence, a cab is your only option.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Can I use ZoomCar for an airport pickup?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Yes, but it requires more planning than a cab. Most major airports (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai) have ZoomCar hub zones. You pick up the car at the hub, drive yourself out, and return it later. For one-way airport drops — park-and-return — a cab is usually more practical. For multi-day trips starting from the airport, ZoomCar hub pickup is efficient.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">What is the minimum booking time for ZoomCar?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar bookings are typically for a minimum of a few hours, with most pricing shown per day. For trips under 2–3 hours, a cab with its per-km pricing may work out more economically. ZoomCar's value improves the longer you use the car in a given day.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Is Zip different from ZoomCar on Zymo?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Yes. Zip is Zymo's own in-house car fleet — a separate inventory from ZoomCar. Both appear in Zymo search results. Zip cars are owned and managed by Zymo, while ZoomCar cars come from ZoomCar's partner network. Comparing both on Zymo gives you the best price and availability for self-drive across the two fleets.</p>
