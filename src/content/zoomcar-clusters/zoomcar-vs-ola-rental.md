    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">What is Ola Rentals?</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Ola Rentals is a chauffeured hourly rental service — not self-drive. You book a package (4 hours / 40km, 8 hours / 80km, or similar tiers), a driver comes with the car, and you are driven for the duration of that package. The driver stays with the vehicle. You are a passenger, not the driver. This is fundamentally different from ZoomCar, where no driver is included — you drive the car yourself, go where you want, stop where you want, and return the car to a hub at the end of the booking.</p>
    <p>Both products are legitimate choices, but they solve different problems. Ola Rentals is a convenience product for city-based trips where you want a dedicated vehicle without the unpredictability of cab queues or surge pricing. ZoomCar is a freedom product for day trips, road trips, and situations where privacy and self-determination matter.</p>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">Cost comparison for a full day</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Ola's 8-hour/80km package is typically priced at ₹1,200–₹1,800 in major metros, with extra km charged at ₹12–₹15/km and extra hours at ₹100–₹150/hour beyond the package. A ZoomCar hatchback daily booking for the same period runs ₹700–₹1,100 with 100–150km included. For a city trip staying under 80km, the Ola 8-hour package and ZoomCar are comparable in cost — but above 80km, ZoomCar's per-km rate is lower and the pricing is more predictable. For road trips (200km or more), ZoomCar with Subscription becomes significantly cheaper than any Ola hourly package structure.</p>

    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">When Ola Rentals makes more sense</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Ola Rentals is the better choice when you do not have a driving licence or prefer not to drive, when you need a reliable dedicated vehicle for city business meetings across multiple stops without worrying about parking, when you are unfamiliar with a city's roads and want a local driver to navigate, and when the trip is entirely within a city and stays under the package km limit. The Ola Rentals model also removes fuel responsibility — you do not need to manage refuelling.</p>
    
    <h2 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:2rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.4rem">When ZoomCar via Zymo makes more sense</h2>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">ZoomCar is the right choice when you have a driving licence and want full control of the vehicle, when you are doing a day trip or road trip beyond city limits, when privacy is important (no driver listening to your conversations), when your trip involves non-standard stops, detours, or overnight drives, and when total cost matters on long-distance trips where per-km Ola charges would be substantial. Zymo shows both ZoomCar and Zip options — Zip is Zymo's own in-house fleet available in select cities with competitive daily rates alongside ZoomCar's wider network.     <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Is ZoomCar cheaper than Ola Rentals for a full day?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">For trips over 80–100km, ZoomCar is typically cheaper. A ZoomCar hatchback at ₹800–₹1,100/day with 100–150km included compares favourably once you exceed Ola Rental's package km limit. For city trips staying under 80km, the cost is comparable — the choice then depends on whether you want to drive yourself (ZoomCar) or be chauffeured (Ola).</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Can I use Ola Rentals for a road trip?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Ola Rentals is designed for city-based hourly use, not multi-day road trips. The hourly package structure becomes expensive for long-distance travel. ZoomCar with Subscription (unlimited km, multi-day) is built for road trips and is significantly more economical for driving 300km or more over multiple days.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Do I need a driving licence for ZoomCar?</h3>
    <p style="font-size:1.05rem;color:#FFFFFF;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:1rem">Yes. ZoomCar requires a valid Indian driving licence (LMV class) and you must be at least 21. Ola Rentals has no licence requirement since you are a passenger. If you do not have a licence, Ola Rentals or a standard cab is your option.</p>
    
    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">What is Zip on Zymo?</h3>

    <h3 style="color:#FFFFFF;margin:1.5rem 0 0.5rem;font-size:1.15rem">Is Ola Rentals available in all cities?</h3>
